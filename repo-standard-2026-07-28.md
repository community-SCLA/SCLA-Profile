**Gaps found during fetching (stated explicitly, not filled in):**

- The settings page (`code.claude.com/docs/en/settings`) gives file locations only; it does not discuss CLAUDE.md size guidance, the request-vs-guarantee distinction, or context costs.
- The support article does not address relocating CLAUDE.md content to skills or other locations.
- No page states a hard line/token limit for CLAUDE.md — every size statement is phrased as a target ("target", "rule of thumb", "aim for … roughly"). The only hard load limit found applies to auto-memory's `MEMORY.md` (200 lines / 25KB), not CLAUDE.md.
