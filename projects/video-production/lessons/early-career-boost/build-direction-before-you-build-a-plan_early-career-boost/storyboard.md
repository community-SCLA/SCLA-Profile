## Design Keys

- **One palette, program identity only in the title lockup.** `navy` / `navy-deep` carries every dark canvas (always radial, never a linear gradient — the motion `ban` exists because H.264 bands on navy), `paper` and `cultured` carry the light ones. The words "Early Career Boost" in beat 1 are the only place the program is named; everything after it is distinguished by illustration, not by colour.
- **Gold ledger — five beats, and these are all of them: 1, 5, 12, 21, 23.** `gold` appears only as the title-lockup rule under the eyebrow (1), the "patterns" underline (5), the affirmed card's left edge (12), the three noticed marks (21), and the closing-lockup rule plus its `cta-label` (23). Five of twenty-three beats holds the accent under one beat in four. Everywhere else structure is `blue` — including the beat-10 arrowhead, the beat-13 stat numeral, the beat-14–16 rail markers, the beat-17 badge baseline and the beat-20 spark, all of which an earlier pass had spending gold. The beat-13 numeral is the specific reason: gold at stat scale on `cultured` reads near 1.9:1, so the largest ink in the video is set in `navy`.
- **Dimming on dark grounds is `blue`, not `muted-video`.** tokens.yml scopes `muted-video` to light grounds ("rail captions on light"), and no dark-canvas dim token exists. Beat 7 therefore dims spent questions from `paper` to `blue` — the nearest named token that recedes from paper on navy while staying well above video contrast. `muted-video` is used only on `paper` grounds, at beats 14–16.
- **Nothing is on screen before it is spoken.** Every list item, card and copy line names the word onset it enters on, taken from `audio/narration.words.json` — the six experience cards (6), the four questions (7), the six value chips (15) and the four ingredient cards (18) each get their own entrance rather than a single impossible tight stagger, and the five title chips split 2/3 across the beat 2–3 pair. No beat holds a static frame longer than about four seconds.
- **A repeating three-part rail is the only recurring component.** Beats 14–16 draw one blue `rule` as a vertical spine, once, and hold it unchanged; three `chip` markers ride it, the live one at full-weight blue and the spent ones at `muted-video`. No `step-node` anywhere in this video. Every other beat changes layout entirely (title lockup, chip fan, chip drain, split frame, card grid, question stack, balance scale, two diptychs, dotted path, quote pair, stat card, badge, ingredient row, convergence, two-lane, portrait, closing lockup) so no two adjacent scenes look alike.
- **Type roles map to function, not to size preference.** `display` only on the title lockup, `heading` on scene assertions, `quote` on the three sentences the lesson wants remembered, `body` on everything read as a sentence, `label` on the eyebrow, chips, step numbers and brandline, `stat` once on the "3" transitional card, `cta-label` once on the closing line.
- **On-screen copy is the spoken key phrase, never the transcript.** Beats 12 and 22 carry only the contrast and the promise the narration lands on; beat 4 carries the governing lead-in "What they do not tell you:" above its three fragments so a muted viewer reads the thesis rather than its inverse; beat 8 keeps the script's own hedges, "often" and "formal". Beat 23's closing line is this script's closing line — nothing about a next-module subject is invented on screen.
- **Text and illustration never share a box.** Captions sit beneath their panels (6, 9, 10, 18), beside them with a full column of air (5, 22), or in the bottom content band above the footer reserve (8) — and multi-card layouts keep the card gutter so a wrapped caption grows into space that already exists. Beat 12's quote cards are 760px, widened from 650px so the affirmed line holds `quote` scale instead of dropping to `body`.
- **Every canvas inversion names its transition.** Beats 2, 5, 6, 7, 8, 12, 17, 18, 20, 22 and 23 each describe how the ground changes — push, seam split, seam sweep, radial flood, wipe-down, gutter straighten, iris, rise, left wipe, sweep-off, four-edge close — so no cut is left to the builder's guess. Beat 11 carries its dots in from the previous scene instead.
- **Motion is one settle per element and one resolve per scene.** Entrances stay in the 0.4–0.6s band with a 0.12–0.18s `stagger` and the whole frame settles inside 1.2s; each scene ends with a single `closing-beat` (a rule completing, an accent popping, dimmed items restoring). The rule-wipe row device is used exactly once, at beat 4 — beat 18's baseline extends per card rather than wiping. No `ambient` loop anywhere in this video, and the outro holds full-frame rather than taking an `exit`.

## Deviations

Implemented deviations from the beats above, recorded by the builder (studio
round 3). Nothing else in the board changed.

- **Beats 2 and 7 — transition onsets rounded forward by 0.02s.** The board's
  own beat table starts beat 2 at 00:02.5 and beat 7 at 00:36.4, but names their
  transition cues at 2.48 ("easy") and 36.38 ("Where"), which fall inside the
  previous beat. A clip cannot paint before its own start, so both transitions
  fire at the beat start instead — 2.50 and 36.55. Every other cue is the exact
  word onset from `audio/narration.words.json`.
- **Beats 14–16 — the rail panel is paper with a hairline border, not a
  `fill-subtle` card.** Blue label type on `fill-subtle` measures 2.87:1, under
  the 3:1 floor `hyperframes check` enforces; these beats' token column names
  `paper` and not `fill-subtle`, so the panel takes the paper ground and keeps
  its `border` hairline. Blue on paper clears at 3.49:1.
- **Beats 15 and 16 — the outgoing panel exits as the new one enters.** The board
  gives each state its own panel but not the swap; without it the rail stands
  alone for the ~1s between the marker change and the next panel's cue. The
  spent panel slides left and fades on the marker change, so the frame is never
  idle.
- **Beat 19 — the four card captions fade out as the cards converge.** At the
  0.55 convergence scale their 40px copy would render at 22px, under the body
  floor. The captions leave over the first second of the move, so the composite
  tile is glyphs only — which is what the board's on-screen column for this beat
  lists (the heading alone).
- **Beat 13 — "the label tracks out" is a transform, not a letter-spacing
  tween.** The closing beat widens the label block on `scaleX` (1 → 1.06);
  tracking is not on the seek-safe animatable set, and the transform reads the
  same on screen.
- **Beat 8 — the third moment-dot lands at 49.90.** The board names two onsets
  ("show" 48.74, "naturally" 49.36) for three dots; the third follows the second
  by 0.54s so the pan keeps working through the line.
