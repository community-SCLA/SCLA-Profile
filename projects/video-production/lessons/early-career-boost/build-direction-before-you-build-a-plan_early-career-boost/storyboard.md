## Design Keys

- **One palette, program identity only in the eyebrow.** `navy` / `navy-deep` carries every dark canvas (always radial, never a linear gradient — the motion `ban` exists because H.264 bands on navy), `paper` and `cultured` carry the light ones. The words "Early Career Boost" in beat 1 are the only place the program is named; everything after it is distinguished by illustration, not by colour.
- **Gold is rationed to seven moments.** `gold` appears only as: the "patterns" underline (4), the activating `point-marker` (6), the arrowhead (9), the affirmed card edge (11), the active `step-node` (13–15), the assistive spark (19–20), and the closing rule (22). Everywhere else structure is `blue`, so the accent still reads as an accent.
- **A repeating three-part rail is the only recurring component.** Beats 13–15 reuse one `step-node` spine with `muted-video` for the inactive states, which lets the module's three deliverables read as one structure; every other beat changes layout entirely (title card, chip cluster, split frame, card grid, question stack, balance scale, diptych, dotted path, quote pair, stat card, ingredient row, outro) so no two adjacent scenes look alike.
- **Type roles map to function, not to size preference.** `display` only on the title card, `heading` on scene assertions, `quote` on the four sentences the lesson wants remembered, `body` on everything read as a sentence, `label` on eyebrows, step numbers and chips, `stat` once on the "3" transitional card.
- **Text and illustration never share a box.** Captions sit beneath their panels (5, 8, 9, 17), beside them with a full column of air (4, 21), or in the bottom content band above the footer reserve (7) — and multi-card layouts keep the card gutter so a wrapped caption grows into space that already exists.
- **Motion is one settle per element and one resolve per scene.** Entrances stay in the 0.4–0.6s band with a 0.12–0.18s `stagger` and the whole frame settles inside 1.2s; each scene ends with a single `closing-beat` (a rule completing, an accent popping, dimmed items restoring); `ambient` breathing is finite yoyo only, and the outro holds full-frame rather than taking an `exit`.
