## Design Keys

- **Gold ledger — beats 1 and 14, and nowhere else.** Gold is the lockup accent only: the rule under the eyebrow on the title card and the rule under the closing line. Because gold is rationed to the two lockups, both recurring components drop the gold in their token definitions and take `navy` fill with a paper numeral instead — `step-node` on activation (5–7) and `point-marker` throughout (8–11). Navy is the nearest named token and it is what already draws each component's ring and numeral, so the substitution changes colour, never geometry.
- **Dim states are canvas-specific.** On the light canvases the unlit routes fall back to `border` hairline weight (4–7) and `muted-video` carries the one greyed line (3) — that is the token's stated home. On the dark canvases nothing uses `muted-video`; the faint routes behind the turn are `blue` at reduced opacity over `navy-deep` (12–13), the nearest token-legal dim treatment available on navy.
- **Palette rhythm is dark → light → dark.** `navy` / `navy-deep` own the title card (1), the turn (12–13) and the close (14). The whole argument — credentials, the fork, the three deep dives, the negotiables — runs on light, where the `step-node` ring and the `point-marker` numeral are actually legible. Inside that long light stretch the canvas alternates tone, `cultured` for the chip band and the fork map (2–7, 11) against `paper` for the deep dives (8–10), and each scene changes layout family, so it never reads as one held frame.
- **Nothing appears before it is spoken.** Every list item — chips (2, 13), option cards (5–7), row markers (11), and the three re-seating blocks (10) — is its own entrance keyed to its own word onset, and `stagger` is reserved for siblings inside a single item (glyph with its chip, circle with its label). Structure may precede speech, copy may not: the fork is drawn with three empty nodes before any option is named (4), and beat 11's three sockets seat before the rule reaches them.
- **The rule-wipe device appears three times, as one row exactly once.** A rule wipes on the two lockups (1, 14) where it is the series form, and the rule-linked row runs at a single beat (11); beat 12's turn resolves by the dim routes settling to equal weight instead, and beat 13's by those routes restoring.
- **Type roles are fixed per scene class:** `display` only on the title card, `heading` for scene headings and the closing line, `quote` for the two lines the narration lands hardest on (3, 12), `body` for card and panel copy, `label` for the eyebrow and the chips. Every declared role carries authored copy in the on-screen column — no slot is left for the builder to fill, and no line runs long enough to need a scale it was not written for.
- **Three recurring components carry the argument.** `step-node` is the map vocabulary (4–7) — three routes, one lit at a time; `point-marker` is the deep-dive vocabulary (8–11) — one numbered idea per scene; `chip` bookends the video, holding what you already have (2) and what the choice must satisfy (13). A viewer learns each shape once.
- **Text and illustration are always in separate bands or separate columns** — glyph row above chip row (2), copy left / path right (3), heading top-left clear of every route (4), text column beside illustration column (8–9), heading band above the panel (10). No beat asks a reader to read across a drawing.
- **Motion follows the tokens contract:** every entrance settles by 1.2s, the outgoing illustration takes its 0.3s `exit` before the incoming one enters (9), and each scene's last beat carries one cued `closing-beat` resolve — a rule completing, an accent landing, or dimmed items restoring — never an in-place re-animation.

## Deviations

Recorded by the builder (studio round 3). Each is the closest faithful version of
the beat as written; no other beat, token or design key changed.

- **Beat 2 — "the third chip's border brightens" is an additive accent ring.** A
  second 2px `blue` ring lands just outside chip 3's border rather than recolouring
  the border, because the `chip` component is *defined* as a 2px blue border and the
  tokens `closing-beat` rule requires an additive resolve, never an in-place
  re-animation. The cue also moved 6.32 → 6.20 so the accent lands before the band's
  6.40 exit rather than under it.
- **Beat 3 — the path starts at the left edge of its own region (x=800), not the
  frame's left edge.** The same beat reserves the clear left third for the question
  copy and puts the path in the right two thirds; a path from the frame edge would
  have run under the copy.
- **Beat 6 — "node 2's fill settles" is a `blue` accent ring landing on node 2** at
  20.40. Node 2's fill is already up from 18.56, and re-animating it in place is
  banned by the tokens motion contract, so the cued resolve is additive.
- **Beats 9 and 10 — the outgoing line and illustration leave with their own
  heading, not when the next one lands.** The promotion body line exits at 33.30
  with its heading and the lateral-move panels exit at 40.30. Holding them to the
  next entrance left "Deepening your influence and scope" sitting under the *Lateral
  move* heading, and the department/function panels sitting under *Role redesign*.
  The storyboard fixes when each new item lands and is silent on when the old one
  leaves; this is the reading that keeps on-screen copy true to the spoken line.
- **Beat 13 — the pull-quote "shortens" by fading its trailing words and
  re-centring, not by re-typesetting.** The quote lifts to the top band as one
  container (rise + scale 0.92) while "in general." fades out and the container
  re-centres on the remaining line. Font size is not a seek-safe animatable property
  in HyperFrames, so a true type-scale change could not be rendered
  deterministically.
