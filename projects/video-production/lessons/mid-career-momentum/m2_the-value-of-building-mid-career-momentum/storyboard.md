## Design Keys

- **Palette rhythm is dark → light → dark.** `navy` / `navy-deep` own the title card (1), the fork map (4–7), the turn (12–13) and the close (14); `paper` / `cultured` / `fill-subtle` own the credential band (2–3) and the three deep-dive scenes (8–11). Alternating the canvas is what keeps fourteen beats from reading as one long scene.
- **Gold is rationed to four moments only** — the title rule (1), the node that is currently lit (5–7), the three-up rule completing (11), and the closing line (14). Every other accent is `blue`, so the gold still reads as emphasis by the time the close arrives.
- **Type roles are fixed per scene class:** `display` only on the title card, `heading` for scene headings, `quote` for the two lines the narration lands hardest on (3, 12), `body` for card and panel copy, `label` for the eyebrow, chips and the brandline. Nothing borrows a role from another class.
- **Three recurring components carry the argument.** `step-node` is the map vocabulary (4–7) — three routes, one lit at a time; `point-marker` is the deep-dive vocabulary (8–11) — one numbered idea per scene; `chip` bookends the video, holding what you already have (2) and what the choice must satisfy (13). A viewer learns each shape once.
- **Text and illustration are always in separate bands or separate columns** — glyph row above chip row (2), copy left / path right (3), heading band above the panel (10). No beat asks a reader to read across a drawing.
- **Motion follows the tokens contract:** every entrance settles by 1.2s with a `stagger` between siblings, the outgoing illustration takes its 0.3s `exit` before the incoming one enters (9), `ambient` breathing is a finite yoyo on beat 4 only, and five beats carry an explicit `closing-beat` resolve — a rule completing or an accent landing — never an in-place re-animation.
