## Design Keys

- **Canvas rhythm is light-heavy in the middle, dark at the three turns.** `navy` / `navy-deep` own the title card (1), the AI-acceleration turn (7–9), the "not to avoid the work" turn (12–13), the two releases (18–20) and the close (25); `paper` / `cultured` / `fill-subtle` own the stereotype row (2–3), the experiment field (4–6), the human-elements panel (10–11), the module rail (14–17) and the question (21–24). Alternating the canvas every three to four beats is what keeps twenty-five beats from reading as one long scene.
- **Gold is rationed to seven landings only** — the title rule (1), the small first step (3), the closing card of the five (5), the completed marker set (9, 11), the lit node on the rail (14–17), the oversized numeral (20) and the closing line (25). Everywhere else the accent is `blue`, so gold still reads as arrival by the time the close lands.
- **Type roles are fixed per scene class:** `display` only on the title card, `stat` only on the single numeral in beat 20, `quote` for the four lines the narration leans on (3, 7, 12, 18–19), `heading` for scene headings, `body` for card and list copy, `label` for eyebrows, chips, the brandline and the numeral's caption. No role is borrowed across classes.
- **Three components carry the argument, each learned once.** `chip` is the "small and plural" vocabulary — the five experiments (6) and the three ways AI helps (13); `point-marker` is the enumerated-list vocabulary (8–9, 11); `step-node` is reserved entirely for the five-module rail (14–17), so the rail reads as the only thing in the video that is a sequence with an end.
- **Every scene reserves a column or band for illustration that text never enters** — glyphs sit above their captions (2, 4–5, 13), illustrations hold the opposite half from the text column (3, 10, 18, 22–24), and the rail cards alternate above and below the rail (14–17) so no two ever share a band. The bottom band stays empty for footer chrome throughout.
- **Motion follows the tokens' contract literally:** every entrance settles inside 1.2s on a 0.12–0.18s stagger with the ease varied per element; outgoing content exits before incoming content lands (3, 10, 19, 21, 23) so two illustrations never coexist; each scene resolves with one cued `closing-beat`; the only ambient movement is the finite bloom in beat 7 — nothing repeats indefinitely, and the dark fields use radial falloff, never a linear gradient.
