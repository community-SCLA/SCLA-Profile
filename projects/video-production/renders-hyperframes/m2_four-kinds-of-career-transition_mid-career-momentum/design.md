# Design — The Graft Row

## Chosen concept

One mature standing stock feeds four planted beds along a single ground rail. The rail shows whether a move was chosen or forced; the carry-run length shows whether the move is adjacent or genuinely new. Solid wood is experience carried forward. Outlined wood is new growth.

## Visual carrier

The stock and rail establish in the opening and remain fixed. Four beds fill in sequence: Pivot, Reinvention, Rebuild, Forced Reinvention. Their recipes read high/low/high/low in carried wood, their runs read short/long/short/long, only the final two rails are breached, and only the fourth form is tied to supports. A final canopy rule resolves all four forms to equal standing height.

## Beat-to-frame map

1. `b01` — The standing stock grows three solid limbs for projects, teams, and stakeholders; the four future beds are only faint stakes.
2. `b02` — The whole ground rail, four dashed beds, short/long run samples, and whole/breached rail samples establish the two questions.
3. `b03` — Pivot seats in bed one from a short run; its carried leaf-tabs turn face-out and one outlined pair caps the form.
4. `b04` — Reinvention grows in bed two from a long run: a small solid base and extensive outlined growth.
5. `b05` — The rail under bed three breaches first; Rebuild then rises from a short run with mostly solid wood.
6. `b06` — The fourth rail breaches, a long run reaches it, support ties stake in, and Forced Reinvention grows mostly in outline.
7. `b07` — A single canopy rule draws across the completed crowns on “None of these four is better.”
8. `b08` — Compact planning labels connect the existing recipes to identity work, grief, and experimentation without changing the row.
9. `b09` — The short/long run and whole/breached rail questions reappear as a concise prompt above the row.
10. `b10` — Three unoccupied foreground stakes mark current role, desired role, and one-year success.
11. `b11` — One solid unplanted cutting rests before the row as the evidence-led next choice.
12. `b12` — The cutting remains unplanted; the Next Move Statement and final clarity line settle over the complete row.

## Motion logic

Every landed stroke is permanent. Entrances add strokes with short draw, rise, or face-out actions and then settle pixel-static. Breaches arrive before their forms. The canopy is the only full-row resolve. The final foreground cutting stays deliberately uncommitted. No ambient loops, swaying, travel along the rail, or in-place reanimation are used.
