# Design — Your Reinvention Roadmap

## Frame

- 1920 × 1080, with a 120px frame inset and a reserved lower footer band.
- Deep navy provides the map field; warm white cards create moments of reflection.
- A persistent compass path links scenes without implying a fixed destination.

## Type

- Proxima Nova, falling back to system sans-serif.
- Headlines: 72–96px, weight 900.
- Supporting copy: 40–48px, weight 400 or 700.
- Labels: 22px, uppercase, tracked.

## Color roles

- Navy: foundation and primary text.
- Blue: direction, learning, and route lines.
- Gold: commitments, active markers, and the final next step.
- Cultured white: quiet working surfaces.

## Motion

- A single paused, seekable timeline controls every transition.
- Elements enter over 0.4–0.6 seconds with short staggered reveals.
- Route drawing, compass rotation, and card lifts are deterministic and tied to lesson time.
- No looping motion. Each beat settles before its narration ends.

## Accessibility

- All essential language appears at 40px or larger.
- Text maintains strong contrast against its surface.
- Motion supports meaning and never relies on color alone.
