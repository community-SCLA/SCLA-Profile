# Your Reinvention Roadmap

## Creative idea

A compass line travels across a practical ninety-day map. Each scene turns an abstract part of reinvention into a clear landmark: direction, three phases, runway, and the next committed step. The line adapts rather than breaking when the route changes, reinforcing that a roadmap is a tool for learning, not a contract.

## Visual progression

1. **Arrival:** A final-module marker lands on the map.
2. **Reframing:** A rigid dotted route flexes into a responsive compass path.
3. **The four elements:** Four distinct landmarks assemble into one roadmap.
4. **Ninety days:** Three thirty-day phases fill in as an achievable planning horizon.
5. **Resolve:** The full route simplifies to one illuminated next step.

## Treatment

Clean editorial motion on warm paper and deep navy, using SCLA blue for direction and gold for commitment. Large, readable type and restrained map geometry keep the lesson calm, supportive, and practical.
