# Concept: The Evidence Gallery

A polished gallery metaphor helps mid-career learners select the experiences that point forward. Each scene moves from an overfilled archive toward a clear, intentional exhibition: a future-facing question, a curated set of work, selection criteria, two practical paths, the RAMS bullet framework, and a confident final gallery wall.

## Learning arc

1. Ask which experiences prove readiness for the desired future.
2. Curate rather than inventory.
3. Filter for strengths, scope, and relevant impact.
4. Apply the filter to leadership and lateral moves.
5. Rewrite three to five experiences with RAMS.
6. Tell a true story whose direction is unmistakable.

## Visual approach

Museum labels, framed evidence cards, and a restrained navy-paper-gold palette make the lesson feel thoughtful and practical. Motion is purposeful: frames settle into place, criteria lock together, and the final selected cards align toward the learner’s next move.
