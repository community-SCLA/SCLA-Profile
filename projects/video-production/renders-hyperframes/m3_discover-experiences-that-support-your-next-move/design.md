# Design: The Evidence Gallery

## Frame

- 1920 × 1080, with a 120px content inset and a quiet 120px footer reserve.
- Paper-white gallery field, navy typography, blue structure, and gold emphasis.
- Proxima Nova with system fallbacks; large, short phrases instead of dense narration on screen.

## System

- Persistent top label: MID-CAREER MOMENTUM / MODULE 3.
- A slim progress rail advances across seven distinct beats.
- Evidence cards use 12px corners, crisp borders, and subtle shadows.
- The recurring gold “NEXT MOVE” marker supplies visual continuity.

## Beat treatments

1. A question mark resolves into a forward arrow and three evidence cards.
2. An overcrowded storage stack is reduced to a deliberate three-frame gallery.
3. Strength, scope, and impact form a three-part selection lens.
4. Leadership proof maps teams, influence, and visible outcomes.
5. Lateral proof connects learning, translation, and collaboration.
6. RAMS builds one bullet in four sequential layers and highlights “3–5 experiences.”
7. Selected frames align on a path toward NEXT MOVE; the final gold line resolves.

## Motion

All motion is driven by one paused, seekable GSAP timeline. Entrances settle within 1.2 seconds, exits clear content without moving the persistent furniture, and no animation repeats infinitely.
