# Who Will Walk This Next Chapter With You?

## Core idea

Turn strategic networking from a crowded contact list into a deliberately chosen constellation. Each relationship becomes a bright point with a clear role, reason, and respectful 90-day next step.

## Visual story

The lesson opens on scattered names and tactical questions, then pulls back to reveal a calm constellation map. Peers, sponsors, opportunity holders, and mentors receive distinct positions around the learner. The map grows deliberately—not busily—until the learner selects one relationship to nurture first.

## Learner takeaway

Choose three to five people who fit the career story you are building, understand why each person matters, and define one realistic outcome for the next 90 days.
