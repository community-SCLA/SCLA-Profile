# Design direction

The lesson is one evolving relationship map, not a sequence of slides. A persistent constellation occupies the visual field: the learner stays at its center while named people, role cards, target-field boundaries, and relationship lines accumulate as the narration earns them. The compact editorial prompt at left changes the question; the map at right records the answer.

The visual progression has five readable acts. Tactics begin as disconnected question chips. The deeper question creates the first relationship, then five distinct people accumulate as trusted peer, pathfinder, opportunity holder, sponsor, and mentor. The same people are read through Position, Relationship, Direction, and Action lenses. Relevance and role-mix checks edit the existing connections rather than replace the map. The final act selects one opportunity holder, illuminates one gold route, and resolves that route into a 0–30–60–90 day path with a specific 20-minute conversation.

Use only the approved SCLA navy, blue, gold, white, pale blue, and muted blue tokens. Proxima Nova is loaded from the frozen local font files. Thin blue links show possibility; a single gold link shows chosen action. Keep meaningful content inside the safe area and above the footer reserve. Motion is seek-safe and purposeful: nodes arrive, links draw, lenses activate, and the chosen relationship resolves. The uninterrupted progress rail and slow constellation rotation maintain temporal continuity without decorative distraction.
