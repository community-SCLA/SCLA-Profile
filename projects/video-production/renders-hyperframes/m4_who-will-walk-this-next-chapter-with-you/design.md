# Design direction

Use the SCLA navy canvas as a quiet night-sky field, with thin blue relationship lines and restrained gold highlights. Large editorial type carries one thought at a time. Rounded white cards represent people; small role chips identify peer, sponsor, opportunity holder, and mentor. Motion should feel like thoughtful mapping: points appear, lines draw, and the chosen relationship resolves in gold. Keep all meaningful content inside the safe area and above the footer reserve.

The composition uses Proxima Nova with the system fallback, white body copy, gold emphasis, and blue connective details. Avoid decorative clutter, full-screen gradients, and perpetual motion.
