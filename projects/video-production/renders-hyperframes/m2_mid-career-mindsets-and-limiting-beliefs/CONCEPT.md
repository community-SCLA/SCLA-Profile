# Mindset Is a Lens, Not a Wall

Six visual beats move from confinement to choice. Limiting beliefs first appear as oversized quotation cards that narrow a bright career path. The frame then opens into leverage, low-risk experiments, and a practical redesign board. The final prompt leaves the learner with one open path and one small next step.

The visual system uses SCLA navy as the stable foundation, blue for possibility, and gold only for decisions and forward motion. Clean editorial diagrams, generous spacing, and restrained motion keep the lesson mature and encouraging rather than playful or prescriptive.
