# Concept: From Task to Future-You Proof

## What
A guided resume-builder walkthrough that turns a flat orientation task into credible, measurable proof of next-level impact.

## So What
The visual story moves from a plain bullet through a four-part lens, an AI-assisted rewrite, an accuracy check, and three career-path filters. The interface is illustrative rather than a product claim.

## Now What
Viewers leave with a focused action: select three to five honest headline stories, then use the same future-facing approach for LinkedIn.

## Visual direction
Clean editorial workspace on warm white and deep navy. Gold marks decisions and evidence; blue signals tools and forward motion. Large type, restrained cards, and a persistent progress rail make the lesson easy to follow.
