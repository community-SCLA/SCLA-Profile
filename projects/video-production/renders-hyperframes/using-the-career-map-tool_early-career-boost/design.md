## Concept angle

> **One squared worksheet sheet, clipped to a board, that gains every mark the
> lesson describes into four fixed regions — and is finally ruled into four
> named zones that re-sort every mark already on it.**

**Persistence: beats b01 → b45, 100% of the runtime.** The sheet, its faint
grid, its clip bar and its four regions are drawn in the opening beat and are
never cleared, never rebuilt and never left. The grid lines the axes finally
land on are the same grid lines that have been under the whole lesson.

**The rule, stated out loud:** *if an element cannot be justified as another way
of reading the same sheet, it does not exist.* Nothing enters this frame except
as a mark ON the sheet — lettering in the header band, a block in the left
intake margin, a tile on the right shelf, or a chip / rule / stroke in the
centre working field. There is no second illustration, no browser chrome, no
cursor, no window frame and no decorative object.

### The four fixed regions (in place from b01, never moved or resized)

| Region | Box (px) | What it ever holds |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Header band | x 200–1720, y 210–300 | the step, hand-lettered — one heading at a time |
| Left intake margin | x 200–560, y 330–900 | profile lines, control blocks, the resume slip |
| Right shelf | x 1300–1720, y 330–560 | the six approach tiles, 3x2, only ever emptying |
| Centre working field | x 600–1260 (upper), x 200–1720 (lower), y 330–900 | pinned tiles, job chips, lanes, the time band, the axes |

### The marks — five shapes only

* **Approach tiles** — wide rounded rectangles, 2px ink outline, pin dot top-left;
  outline while shelved, filled once pinned.
* **Job chips** — narrower rectangles with one interior rule line; they grow
  taller as detail lines are added, they are never swapped for new props.
* **Control blocks** — small solid blocks in the left margin carrying only
  button words the narration speaks.
* **The resume slip** — a rectangle with one folded corner, clipped over the
  margin's lower edge.
* **Ruled strokes** — path lanes, the time-frame band, and the two heavy axis
  strokes, all drawn along grid lines that already exist.

## Milestone frames — the accumulation contract

Re-pinned to real beats. The countable state is named; a beat that changes none
of these numbers does not get to exist.

**b01–b11 (0–25%) — Started.** Header band lettered with the landing step. Right
shelf carries **six** labelled approach tiles with one gap where a tile has been
lifted out. Centre field: pinned tiles, alone, surrounded by open grid. Left
margin: control blocks and the first ruled profile lines. The lower two-thirds
is bare grid.
*Countables: shelf gapped · pinned tiles present · margin marks 1–4 · lower field empty.*

**b12–b22 (25–50%) — Fed.** Same sheet, same regions, more weight. The pinned
tiles have not multiplied — they have **thickened**: each carries a stack of
ruled detail lines, so each is two to three times its b09 height. The margin has
filled into an intake column, with the dog-eared resume slip clipped over its
lower corner. **The centre lower two-thirds is still provably bare grid.** The
one honest subtraction lands here: on b17 the pinned marks dim to bare outline
while the confirmation sits over the sheet, because the script says plainly that
regenerating removes the old approaches and jobs.
*Countables: margin marks 6 · chips carry 3–4x their b09 ink · lower field still empty.*

**b23–b34 (50–75%) — Sorted.** The lower two-thirds becomes the loudest part of
the frame: ruled path lanes with job chips strung along them, a quick-stats
strip at each lane's left end, one lane bracketed as the selected path, and a
ruled time-frame band along the bottom with the selected lane's chips stepping
upward across it, each taller than the one before.
*Countables: lanes ruled · many job chips in the lower field · one lane bracketed · staircase climbing.*

**b35–b45 (75–100%) — Judged.** Two heavy strokes cross the centre field on grid
lines that have been there since b01, labelled with the script's own wording.
Four zones named in their corners. **Every chip that arrived over the whole
video is unpinned and re-placed as a plotted mark inside one of the four zones.**
The lane rules survive as faint ghosts; the shelf is empty.
*Countables: shelf 0 · lanes ghosted · every chip at a new coordinate · four zones named.*

Bare, fed, sorted, judged. Strip every heading and the four still restore to
order from the pictures alone: the shelf only ever empties, the field only ever
fills.

## The payoff beat

**b30 — "Plot each option you are considering on two axes."** The two heavy
strokes rule straight across marks that are already on the sheet. Up to that
instant the sheet is a form read top to bottom; from that stroke on it is a
field read by position. The faint grid that sat quietly under the whole lesson
re-reads as a coordinate field, and every loose mark the viewer watched arrive
one at a time re-reads as one comparable set. A quieter re-read lands earlier at
b22, when loose pinned chips become connected lanes — it teaches the viewer that
this sheet re-sorts, which is what earns b30.

The frame at b01 is a blank ruled sheet with a full shelf. The closing frame is
that same sheet, and it could not have been drawn at b01 — not by adding a
heading, only by everything in between.

## Lettering discipline (grafted from the runner-up pitch, carried whole)

* **Every word lettered on the sheet is a word the narration speaks** — the two
  axis labels, the four zone names, the six approach names, the profile fields
  and the button words. Nothing else gets lettered.
* **Never letter a count, total or figure.** The script's path/job numbers and
  its five-year figure appear inside illustrative phrasing; they are what the
  tool *might* return, never content to draw. Quantity is shown by the number of
  marks actually drawn, never written down. The summary mark is a ruled band with
  no numerals; the time-frame band is ruled with plain ticks.
* **No screen feature the script does not describe.** No chrome, no cursor, no
  invented panel, no invented control.

## Two arrivals per beat

Every beat lands its heading (or its first mark) on the beat's opening words and
a **second, different arrival in its back half**, cued off that beat's own word
timings — a tile leaving the shelf, a detail line thickening a chip, a lane
being ruled, a zone being named. No beat's picture is finished at its midpoint,
and no two consecutive beats draw the same illustration: the guard from
CONCEPT.md holds — **no two consecutive beats may change the same countable.**

## Palette

Values come from `tokens.yml colors:` (loaded by `check_brand.py`, which fails
any literal that is not one of them at any alpha). Roles for this video:

| Role | Token | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Page ground | `navy-deep` | full-bleed background child, never the root |
| Ground lift | `navy` | radial lift behind the board |
| The sheet | `cultured` | the worksheet paper itself |
| Grid + hairlines | `border` | the squared grid, region rules, ghosted lanes |
| Ink | `ink` | every lettered word on the sheet, chip outlines |
| Structure | `blue` | region labels, lane rules, the axis strokes |
| Focus accent | `gold` | **reserved**: what the sheet is pointing at right now — the newest mark, the selected lane, the named zone |

One accent hue with one job. Gold never decorates.

## Type

Proxima Nova only, the vendored woff2 400/700/900 in `assets/fonts/`. Hierarchy
is weight, size and colour — never a second family.

| Role | Size | Weight |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Lesson title (title card) | 104px | 900 |
| Beat heading (header band) | 60px | 900 |
| Body / detail on the sheet | 40–44px | 400/700 |
| Tile + chip label | 40px | 700 |
| Label / eyebrow / zone name | 26px | 700, uppercase, 0.14em |

Body floor is the tokens.yml floor (40px); nothing viewer-read sits below it.
Headings are `data-role="heading"`, Title Case, no terminal period.

## Frame

* 1920x1080. Nothing content-bearing crosses the safe area, the frame padding or
  the content-bottom band — all three LOADED from `tokens.yml` and graded on real
  pixels by `check_ink.py`.
* The board sits at x 150–1770, y 165–935, so every mark is inside the
  120px padding ring and above the 960px content-bottom line.
* The program eyebrow is the only element in the top keep-out, inside the
  `chrome-regions` rectangle `tokens.yml` declares by name.

## Motion

* Entrances only: 0.35–0.6s, `power3.out` / `back.out(1.4)`, pinned to the word
  timestamp that earns them. Nothing settled re-animates in place.
* The sheet is only ever *marked, thickened, re-ruled or re-placed* — never
  rebuilt. The one permitted re-placement is the final re-sort into the zones,
  and it is a one-way move.
* One ambient breath, on the background glow alone, declared inline with
  `/* motion-allow: … */`. No idle drift on anything on the sheet.
