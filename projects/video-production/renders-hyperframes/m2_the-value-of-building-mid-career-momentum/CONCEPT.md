# The Momentum Engine

## Core idea

Mid-career experience is stored energy, but activity alone cannot aim it. The
lesson turns that stored experience into visible direction through a sequence of
mechanisms: noise obscures a blank decision, drift loops without a destination,
a named move straightens the route, small steps compound, three materially
different paths emerge, and three real-life filters reveal fit.

## Explanatory carrier

One gold decision token carries the lesson. It begins buried inside accumulated
experience, gets pulled between frustration and ambition, becomes a named move,
passes through pause / reassess / choose, and finally anchors a 90-day goal. Blue
structures show the available system; gold shows the learner's deliberate choice.

## Beat progression

1. **Stored experience:** projects, teams, and stakeholders stack into a large
   base while the future remains an unresolved fork.
2. **Noise versus paper:** frustration and ambition pull in opposite directions;
   the decision token stays suspended above an empty page.
3. **Drift versus direction:** busy activity circulates in a closed loop. A named
   move breaks that loop into a route, then pause / reassess / choose locks it in.
4. **Compounding and decay:** small steps visibly build on one another; an
   untouched chain loses continuity, showing why one realistic move matters more
   than solving everything.
5. **Three paths:** one starting role branches into three different changes:
   deeper scope in place, a move across contexts, or a redesigned role boundary.
6. **Fit:** the same three options pass through values, energy, and real-life
   constraints. No route wins universally; fit creates the selection.
7. **Resolution:** the chosen path becomes a named move, anchored to a 90-day
   goal, with small steps extending toward the six-to-twelve-month horizon.

## Milestone frames

- A massive experience base with a small unresolved future fork.
- A closed drift loop contrasted with a straight deliberate route.
- A causal chain: named move → small steps → compounding direction.
- Three paths that change different dimensions of the same role.
- A fit filter that produces one chosen route without ranking the others.
- A final transformation from scattered noise into a named, buildable move.

## Motion logic

Motion must demonstrate causality. Experience blocks assemble; opposing forces
pull; paths draw; a deliberate choice displaces drift; steps lock together; a gap
breaks continuity; branches change shape; fit gates align; noise collapses into a
single named move. Generic entrances support these mechanisms but never replace
them.

## Risk and response

The main risk is turning the model into another repeated diagram. Each sentence
therefore changes the carrier's state or relationship, while related sentences
remain in the same visual world. No progress rail, recurring staircase card, or
decorative arrow is used.
