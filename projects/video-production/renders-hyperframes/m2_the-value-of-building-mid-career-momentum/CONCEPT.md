# The Momentum Map

## Core idea

Mid-career momentum is a deliberate move from noise and drift toward one named,
realistic direction. A single gold route travels across the composition, turning
experience into a choice and then into a practical 90-day goal.

## Visual story

1. **Experience, but less clarity:** a field of completed-work markers gathers
   behind a professional while the path ahead branches.
2. **Noise stays in the head:** frustration and ambition orbit as loose thoughts;
   an empty page waits below.
3. **Momentum creates direction:** a drifting route straightens through pause,
   reassess, choose, and small compounding steps.
4. **Three paths:** promotion, lateral move, and role redesign become three clear
   routes on one map.
5. **What each path changes:** each route receives a distinct, concrete outcome.
6. **The fit test:** values, energy, and life constraints overlap to reveal fit.
7. **Named next move:** the chosen route resolves into a 90-day goal and a final
   sequence of small steps.

## Art direction

Editorial motion graphics with map-like lines, crisp cards, oversized typography,
and restrained geometric illustration. Navy carries reflection and uncertainty;
paper backgrounds carry choices and action. Gold always represents the deliberate
next move, while blue supports structure and options.

## Motion language

One deterministic GSAP timeline reveals every beat. Lines draw forward, cards
settle into ordered grids, and particles converge rather than wander. Transitions
reuse the gold route so the lesson feels like one continuous decision journey.
