# Testing Your Next Chapter

## Core idea

Career change becomes clearer through small, deliberate experiments—not through reflection alone. The lesson moves from a closed “thinking loop” into an open evidence-gathering field, then resolves as three practical experiment cards.

## Visual story

- **Act 1 — The loop:** A circular path and stacked reflection prompts show why introspection can keep a person inside what they already know.
- **Act 2 — New evidence:** The loop opens into a field of signals: feedback, texture, energy, people, and real day-to-day experience.
- **Act 3 — Three experiments:** Informational interview, trial project, and shadow day appear as distinct, numbered field tests.
- **Act 4 — One-question discipline:** A large question card narrows the work to one answerable question and reframes contradictory evidence as a successful result.
- **Close:** The three experiments lock into a simple route toward a tested next chapter.

## Motion language

One seekable GSAP timeline drives the full lesson. Cards rise and settle, evidence nodes connect, and the thinking loop opens into a forward path. Motion is purposeful and finite; no element loops infinitely.
