# Design direction

## Frame
1920 × 1080. Deep navy stage with a restrained network-grid texture, generous 120px margins, and a permanent lesson label in the top-left chrome zone.

## Type
Proxima Nova with system fallback. Display language is bold and compact; supporting language is at least 40px. Small uppercase labels are at least 22px.

## Color
Navy is the field, paper is the primary type color, blue carries connective information, and gold marks the active signal. No full-frame linear gradients.

## Visual system
The gold signal dot and connecting route persist across scenes. Cards use subtle translucent navy-light fills with crisp blue borders. Numbered markers introduce the four building blocks. The final scene condenses the system into three referable outcomes.

## Motion
One paused GSAP timeline controls the entire composition. Scenes use short opacity, translate, scale, and route-draw entrances with restrained stagger. No infinite animation. All state is deterministic and seekable.
