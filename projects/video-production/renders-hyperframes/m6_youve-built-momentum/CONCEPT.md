# You've Built Momentum

## Core idea

The lesson turns the learner's completed work into a visible system. A single gold momentum line travels through the composition, connecting the target, materials, network, AI-aware direction, and Momentum Snapshot before resolving into a forward path.

## Visual story

- **Opening:** a quiet pause, then the completed Mid-Career Momentum path comes into focus.
- **Proof:** Modules 2–6 appear as distinct milestones connected by one continuous line.
- **Reframe:** the milestones assemble into a four-part system: outcome, visibility, relationships, and results.
- **Keep it moving:** three large numbered reminders show how to maintain, reuse, and share the system.
- **Close:** possible next moves converge into one compounding path and a clear invitation to continue.

## Tone

Earned, warm, and forward-looking. The composition celebrates real progress without confetti or inflated claims. Motion is steady and cumulative: each beat leaves something useful behind for the next.

## Motion principle

One paused, seekable GSAP timeline controls all reveals. Cards rise into place, rules draw from left to right, and the momentum line advances only as the narration progresses. No infinite animation or autoplay loop is used.
