# The Through-Line

Career repositioning is shown as a clear line connecting past proof to a future role. Three professional case studies—Alex, Jordan, and Priya—build the idea before a four-step path turns it into an action plan.

## Visual approach

- A continuous gold through-line travels through the lesson and resolves into an open doorway.
- Each case study uses a distinct evidence card while staying within one consistent SCLA system.
- Short, editorial phrases support the narration rather than duplicating it on screen.
- The final sequence gathers direction, story, language, and signals into one future-facing profile.

## Motion approach

One paused, seekable GSAP timeline controls all movement. Cards enter with short vertical moves, the through-line draws from left to right, and scene exits finish before the next beat. Motion is restrained, deterministic, and readable.
