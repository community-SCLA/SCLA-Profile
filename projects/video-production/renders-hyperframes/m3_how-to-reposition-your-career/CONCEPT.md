## Visual thesis
Career repositioning is not a moving route or a clean break. It is a full-frame translation table with three stable columns: **Past evidence**, **What it proves**, and **Future signal**. The same true experience remains in view while its meaning is clarified in the language of the target role.

## Milestone frames
1. The “start completely over” myth is crossed out without erasing past experience.
2. Existing experience is reframed so it points toward a future role.
3. Alex’s operations evidence becomes strategy proof and a credible Strategy Leader signal.
4. Jordan’s teaching evidence becomes learning-design proof and a Learning Experience Designer signal.
5. Priya’s informal leadership becomes measurable people-leadership proof.
6. The table condenses into four moves: clarify, curate, translate, and update public signals.
7. The final table preserves the true past while making the next role obvious.

## Motion
The table stays fixed at frame scale. Evidence enters first, the proof translation resolves second, and the future signal settles last. Named connector rules show only real relationships. Metrics and rewritten phrases appear when narrated. There is no moving marker, decorative dot, route progress, module numbering, current/total counter, or false interface motion.
