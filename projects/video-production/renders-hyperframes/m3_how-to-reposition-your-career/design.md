## Type, color, and spacing
Use only the local Proxima Nova and token palette. Headings are 72–108px; teaching phrases are 36–48px; labels are 20–24px. Navy is the canvas, cultured paper holds evidence, blue identifies translated proof, and gold identifies credible future signals. Table cells use generous padding and no more than two short phrases each.

## Motion logic
One paused GSAP timeline controls sixteen complete visual scenes over the existing 35 audio units. Scenes persist across complete thoughts. Within each table state, evidence establishes, proof translates, and the future signal settles. Connector rules scale only between named states; rewritten phrases and metrics reveal at the corresponding narration. No progress metaphor, route marker, ambient tracker, or ornamental loop is used.
