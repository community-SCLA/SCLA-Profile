# Design: The Through-Line

## Frame

The 1920×1080 canvas uses a 120px outer inset and reserves the lower 120px for captions. A slim program label, lesson number, and progress line remain stable while the main content changes.

## Type and color

Proxima Nova is used throughout, with system sans-serif fallback. Headlines use navy or paper at 72–96px; supporting text stays at 40px or above. Navy provides authority, blue signals forward direction, and gold marks evidence and progress.

## Scenes

1. **Reframe:** “START OVER?” separates, then reconnects as “REPOSITION.”
2. **Proof:** existing experience flows toward a future-role marker.
3. **Alex:** an operations card reveals strategy evidence and a 25% result.
4. **Jordan:** classroom language translates into learning-design language.
5. **Priya:** informal leadership moments assemble into a people-leader profile.
6. **Pattern:** four large numbered steps form a connected path.
7. **Resolve:** the through-line reaches an open doorway labeled “NEXT.”

## Accessibility and motion

Contrast remains high, important text is never conveyed by color alone, and decorative motion does not loop. Every beat has one clear focal point and settles early enough to read.
