# The Story That Makes the Change Legible

## Concept

Turn a career pivot into a clear visual bridge. The lesson begins with a question
hanging between an old role and a new one, then builds a three-part story across
that bridge: backstory, turning point, and forward case. Three warning cards expose
the common failure modes before the bridge resolves into a concise, confident
one-line pitch.

## Visual system

- **Metaphor:** a route line connects past experience to future value.
- **Structure:** editorial cards, numbered nodes, and short bridge statements.
- **Emphasis:** gold marks decisions and key claims; blue carries the route forward.
- **Motion:** one deterministic GSAP timeline reveals each beat and its supporting
  elements, with no looping animation.
- **Accessibility:** large high-contrast type, restrained text per frame, and no
  meaning conveyed by color alone.

## Beat map

1. The external question
2. Why pivots land or unravel
3. Narrative coherence
4. A story that connects past and future
5. The three-part structure
6. Backstory
7. Turning point
8. Forward case
9. Three failure modes
10. Do not apologize
11. Do not erase the old career
12. Name the bridge
13. Build and install the story
