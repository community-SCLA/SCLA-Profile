# From History to Signal — Concept Selection

## Lens A — The Proof Beam (selected)

A single oversized career document fills the frame. At first it is a dense archive: many broad history bands with no obvious destination. A calm blue scan window then isolates one band at a time. The chosen line is rewritten in place, changing from an assigned duty into measurable proof, and a gold beam extends from that proof toward one large future-role target. The same document, scan window, proof line, and target evolve throughout the lesson.

| Criterion | Score | Reason |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Fidelity | 5/5 | Directly expresses archive, scan, rewrite, curate, and forward signal without adding a new claim. |
| Evolution | 5/5 | One document visibly changes from dense history to selected evidence to a future-facing signal. |
| Attention | 5/5 | One large proof line is the focal point; spoken list items are emphasized one at a time. |
| Feasibility | 5/5 | Large HTML type, rectangles, and SVG paths are deterministic and reuse the exact existing timing. |
| **Total** | **20/20** | **Selected: clearest hierarchy and lowest collision risk.** |

## Lens B — The Career Constellation

Each past experience is a large point in a sparse field. Relevant points brighten, connect, and form an arrow-shaped constellation aimed at a future-role star. Results, scope, and alignment become three named anchor points; curation removes dim points.

| Criterion | Score | Reason |

## Selected thesis

**Turn one oversized career document into a proof beam: scan less, say more, and make the future unmistakable.**

## Explanatory hierarchy

1. One short heading names the idea.
2. One large document state demonstrates it.
3. One active phrase, result, or move receives emphasis.
4. Supporting labels stay secondary and never compete with the carrier.

## Milestone frames

1. **Archive:** one oversized page is packed with undifferentiated history bands; the destination is faint.
2. **Scan:** one large aperture examines the page for Results, Scope, and Alignment, one criterion at a time.
3. **Problem:** a duty statement fills the active line but produces no forward beam; a current-role label points backward.
4. **Rewrite:** that same line changes in place to “Led cross-functional project · onboarding −30%”; the proof beam activates.
5. **Three moves:** three large operations happen to the same page in sequence—Select, Rewrite, Point forward.
6. **Curate:** nonessential history bands recede, leaving only a few relevant proof lines and a believable Next Move Statement.
7. **Resolve:** the document and LinkedIn profile align into one clear gold beam that reaches FUTURE YOU and settles on YES.

## Motion language

Use restrained, meaning-led motion: a scan window travels between fixed rows; inactive rows dim; text swaps within one stable proof line; the gold beam draws only when evidence supports the destination; and the final target resolves once. No orbiting, stacks of mini-cards, persistent progress devices, pulsing text, or decorative movement that competes with the teaching point.
