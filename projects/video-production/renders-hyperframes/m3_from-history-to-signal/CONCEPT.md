# From History to Signal

## Core idea
Turn a dense career archive into a clear forward signal. The visual story begins with accumulated records, isolates evidence of impact, then resolves into a focused path toward the next role.

## Visual metaphor
A signal line travels through every scene. It begins buried under document rows, becomes a bright measure of results and scope, and finishes as a direct route from **NOW** to **NEXT**.

## Learning promise
Viewers will recognize three practical moves: select forward-pointing experience, rewrite duties as measurable impact, and align LinkedIn language with the future they want.

## Treatment
Editorial motion graphics with generous white space, navy structure, blue data marks, and gold emphasis. Text is concise and supports the narration rather than transcribing it on screen.
