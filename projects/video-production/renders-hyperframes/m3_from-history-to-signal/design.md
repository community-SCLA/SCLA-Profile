# Design — From History to Signal

## Direction
A clean editorial dashboard that changes from archive density to signal clarity. A persistent top rail names the module; a thin gold signal line and step marker establish progress.

## Palette and type
Use token colors only: deep navy backgrounds, white/cultured panels, blue structure, and gold for the evidence that points forward. Proxima Nova is the preferred family with the token fallback. Large display text stays between 72 and 112 px; supporting copy is at least 40 px.

## Scene system
1. **Archive:** stacked record rows obscure the destination.
2. **Scan:** three cards isolate results, scope, and alignment.
3. **Rewrite:** a duty statement transforms into a measurable outcome.
4. **Three moves:** trajectory, impact, and future-you appear as a connected sequence.
5. **Curate:** selected evidence joins a line from NOW to NEXT.
6. **Question:** the final prompt resolves into a confident YES.

## Motion
One paused seekable GSAP timeline drives all changes. Each beat enters within 1.2 seconds with opacity, vertical movement, line drawing, or card scale. Exits clear content while the top rail and progress line remain. No infinite animation.

## Accessibility
High contrast, large type, short on-screen phrases, meaningful grouping, and no information carried by color alone.
