# Design: One Smart Experiment

## Visual direction

Use the SCLA lesson system as an editorial workshop board: navy grounds establish authority, white and cultured-paper cards keep information approachable, blue connects ideas, and gold marks the learner's next action. The recurring experiment marker evolves from a small dot into a complete target.

## Type and hierarchy

- Proxima Nova, with the supplied system fallback.
- Display statements: 84–110px, weight 900.
- Scene headings: 58–68px, weight 900.
- Supporting language: 36–44px, weight 400.
- Labels: 20–24px, uppercase, generous tracking.
- Keep all meaningful text above 40px except declared labels.

## Composition system

- 1920 × 1080 canvas with a 120px outer frame.
- Persistent top-left program label and top-right lesson title.
- Gold rule provides continuity between scenes.
- Cards use 12px corners, thin blue-gray borders, and strong spacing.
- Diagrams favor one clear relationship over decorative detail.

## Persistent carrier

**The carrying object is the gold experiment marker and its horizontal rule.**
The rule stays in the same frame position while each scene reorganizes the next
small experiment around gold nodes, numbered markers, and the final target. The
composition advances by adding meaning inside that shared workshop field rather
than repainting every narration unit as a new slide.

## Motion

- Each beat enters as a short, ordered reveal and settles within 1.2 seconds.
- Use opacity, translation, scale, and line growth only.
- Transitions are direct fades; no perpetual motion.
- The final beat resolves additively as three focus rings converge on the experiment marker.
- Lists are narration-led sequences, not prefilled boards. Each experiment,
  AI support, human-core quality, roadmap step, and closing focus item enters
  and receives the gold active treatment when its spoken cue arrives.
- The active item stays visually dominant long enough to read; no group-wide
  border sweep is used as a substitute for point-by-point emphasis.

## Frame balance

- Teaching objects occupy the full workshop field: cards and diagrams extend
  through the middle and lower content zones instead of sitting in a small top
  strip.
- Split compositions give copy and visuals comparable weight. The 120px frame
  and footer reserve remain clear while the usable interior is intentionally
  filled.

## Accessibility

- Maintain high contrast on both light and navy backgrounds.
- Avoid placing body copy in the reserved footer region.
- Do not rely on color alone: labels and spatial grouping reinforce every distinction.
