# One Smart Experiment

Entrepreneurship is reframed from a dramatic leap into a practical sequence of small, human-centered experiments. The visual story begins with the familiar myth of the high-risk founder, breaks that myth into approachable starting points, and ends with one focused question the learner can carry into the rest of the track.

## Story arc

1. **Shrink the leap:** contrast the startup myth with a smaller first move.
2. **Make it tangible:** show five credible ways an experiment can begin.
3. **Put AI in its place:** present AI as useful support around a human core.
4. **Name the principle:** use AI to reduce friction, not avoid the work.
5. **Preview the path:** map the concrete work learners will complete.
6. **Lower the stakes:** replace a forever-business decision with one experiment.
7. **Focus the question:** converge on one offer, one group, and one realistic way.

The composition uses clean editorial diagrams, restrained kinetic type, and a recurring gold experiment marker. It contains no generated or stock media; every visual is authored directly in HTML and CSS.
