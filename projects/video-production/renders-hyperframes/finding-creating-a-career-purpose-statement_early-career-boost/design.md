# design.md — brand truth for `finding-creating-a-career-purpose-statement_early-career-boost`

Design source of record for this one video. Brand values are LOADED from this
workspace's own `tokens.yml` copy (colors, type floors, spacing, the pinned
Oxana voice); nothing here restates a hex value a gate also reads.

Lesson: `lesson-scripts/early-career-boost/ready/finding-creating-a-career-purpose-statement.txt`
(program `early-career-boost`, banner "Early Career Boost").
Lane: freeform — the HTML is the authored artifact. No templates, no compiler.
Concept contract: `_concepts/<stem>/CONCEPT.md` (judge-selected, Pitch B with
Pitch A's asymmetric third ingredient grafted in).

## Concept angle

> A field of blank note-scraps re-sorts into three feeder columns and empties
> upward into ONE dashed statement strip, which fills, is labelled a second
> way, hung with qualities, tightened, copied into three rough drafts, and
> finally mounted with a revision loop ruled back at it — the board is on
> screen from beat 1 to beat 29 and nothing on it is ever cleared.

**Persistence: beats b01 → b29, 100% of the runtime.** The scrap field arrives
under the title card in b01 and the same board is on frame for every remaining
beat. Every element the lesson names is another way of reading that board — a
mark IN the field, a slot IN the strip, a chip ON its rail, a tag OUTSIDE its
crop bracket, or a rule drawn AROUND the whole thing.

**The rule, stated out loud:** *if an element cannot be justified as another
way of reading the same board, it does not exist.* There is no second
illustration on any frame and no decorative object anywhere.

### Sharpened against real timings (declared, not silent)

CONCEPT.md's accumulator 5 says "the strip relocates onto a mount on the last
beat". At the real type sizes the heading zone ends at y≈222 and the strip's
head sits at y=276, so a lift would push the mount into the heading. The mount
is therefore realised as an **arriving** element — a gold bracket with an
up-tick ruled directly above the strip on b29, with the loop's arrowhead
landing on the strip in the same beat. Same two-things-resolve-together
payoff, same monotone accumulation (nothing moves away, one more thing is
there), one fewer geometry move. The right-edge notch on "brief" (b21) stays
as the object's one dimensional change.

## The accumulation contract — what the snapshot grid must prove

Five monotone counters. Nothing is ever cleared, so any two stills can be put
back in order by counting alone.

| Counter | b01 | b07 | b12 | b21 | b29 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Column holes (scraps spent) | 0 | 0 | 2 | 8 | 14 (all outlined from b28) |
| Strip interior | absent | empty + 3 ticks | 3 slots filled | + second label row | + ownership rule |
| Chips on the rail | 0 | 0 | 0 | 5 | 5 |
| Struck-through margin tags | 0 | 0 | 0 | 2 | 2 |
| Object geometry | — | — | — | right edge in one notch | mount + closed loop |

Milestone frames, scaled to the synthesized wav (141.489s total) and named by
the beat the snapshot grid actually lands on:

- **25% — 35.4s, beat b06** — full mess, empty container. 24 blank scraps in
  an unsorted drift; the dashed two-rule strip ruled empty above them with its
  crop bracket, revise arc, "?" tag and two gold condition clamps. 0 slots
  ticked, 0 filled, 0 chips, 0 tags, 0 holes. (Grid still: 33.25s.)
- **50% — 70.7s, beat b13** — order below, a filled line above. The same
  scraps now stand in three aligned 2×4 stacks under three labels; the strip
  has its division ticks and its three slots are filled and connected; the two
  struck-through tags land in the right margin. Same marks as 25%, completely
  different geometry. (Grid still: 68.97s. The pure "ordered but still empty"
  state runs b07–b11, 36–59s.)
- **75% — 106.1s, beat b23** — filled, labelled twice, hung with all five
  chips, right edge pulled in one notch, two tags in the margin, first rough
  draft on the right, columns visibly gap-toothed (10 holes). Dense at the
  top, thinning at the bottom — the inverse of 25%. (Grid still: 106.11s.)
- **100% — b29** — the whole board at once: three rough drafts on the right
  with one circled, 14 outlined holes in the columns, the closed loop ruled
  round the lower margin with its arrowhead on the strip, and the mount
  bracket at the head of the board. (Grid still: 136.49s.)

Measured on the gate's own snapshot grid, the board's ink rises monotonically
from 20,968 edge px at b01 to 131,834 at b29 — the accumulation is not a
claim, it is in the pixels.

**The asymmetric third ingredient (grafted from Pitch A).** Slots 1 and 2 are
promoted UP out of columns 1 and 2 and leave holes. Slot 3 — "the strengths
and skills you are *growing toward*" — is not in the work already done, so it
arrives into the strip from OFF the board's right edge, drawn faint, and
column 3 loses no scrap at that moment. Column 3 pays later, at the drafts.

**The judge's guard, honoured.** The three drafts are individually
distinguishable at contact-sheet scale and are drawn rougher than the strip
they copy: draft 1 has an uneven baseline, draft 2's rule overruns its box,
draft 3's falls visibly short. From b17 to b27 exactly one more scrap leaves
the columns every beat, so no two stills in the last third can show the same
column geometry.

## Palette

Every value comes from `tokens.yml colors:` (loaded by `check_brand.py`, which
fails any literal that is not one of them at any alpha).

| Role | Token | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Page ground | `navy-deep` | full-bleed background child, never the root |
| Ground lift | `navy` | radial lift behind the board |
| Foreground | `paper` | headings, promoted statement copy |
| Body / secondary | `paper` at 0.72–0.82 | column labels, tag copy |
| Structure | `blue` | the strip outline, scraps, rails, ticks, drafts, the loop |
| Focus accent | `gold` | **rationed**: the crop bracket, the ownership rule, the selection ring, the mount |

Gold has one job — *what the board is pointing at right now*. It never
decorates.

## Type

Proxima Nova only, the vendored `woff2` 400/700/900 in `assets/fonts/`.
Hierarchy is weight, size and colour — never a second family.

| Role | Size | Weight |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Lesson title (title card) | 76px | 900 |
| Beat heading | 62px | 900 |
| Promoted statement copy | 40px | 700 |
| Column label / tag / connective | 40px | 400 |
| Second label row | 26px | 700, uppercase, 0.14em |
| Quality chip | 22px | 700, uppercase, 0.14em |
| Program eyebrow | 26px | 700, uppercase, 0.16em |

Body floor is the tokens.yml floor (40px); nothing viewer-read sits below it.
The scraps carry **no type at all**, so the field can never breach the floor.
Headings are `data-role="heading"` (or `<h1>`), Title Case, no terminal period.

## Frame

- 1920×1080. All ink sits inside x 190…1790 and y 140…946 — clear of the
  safe-area (72px), frame-padding (120px) and content-bottom (960px) bands
  that `check_ink.py` grades on real pixels.
- Heading zone y 140…222, one line, never two.
- The board: strip y 276…496 (x 240…1440), chip rail y 508…574, column labels
  y 600…700, scrap columns y 712…930, drafts and margin tags x 1500…1780,
  the closed loop ruled at x 196 / y 946 / x 1462.
- The program eyebrow is the only element in the top keep-out band, inside the
  `chrome-regions` rectangle `tokens.yml` declares by name.

## Motion

- Entrances only, 0.35–0.6s, `power3.out` / `back.out(1.5)`, pinned to the
  word timestamp that earns them. Nothing settled re-animates in place.
- Departures are one-shot: a spent scrap fades and drops 10px, once.
- The board is only ever *added to, re-grouped or marked* — never rebuilt.
- One ambient breath on the background glow alone, declared inline with
  `/* motion-allow: … */`. No other repeating tween exists in this build.
- Idle drift, where it exists at all, is translate-only — `scale` + SVG
  `opacity` together ghost in the streaming encode.
