# Welcome and Using Career Transitions as Leaps Ahead

## Core idea

A career transition is not a gap to hide or a single jump to survive. It is a
crossing: a long, human process that can become a deliberate leap forward when
the learner names where they are, tests what comes next, and builds a runway.

## Visual system

One continuous route travels from **SHIFT** to **NEXT CHAPTER**. The route
changes shape as the narration changes meaning: it breaks open, stretches into
a five-year horizon, becomes a permission slip, passes through the three SCLA
tracks, and resolves as a launch runway. A gold traveler marker advances along
the route so the learner always feels forward motion.

The recurring route makes the lesson feel like one authored film rather than a
stack of slides. Text is reserved for short anchors, labels, and the few numbers
that deserve emphasis. The narration carries the detail.

## Beats

1. `ct-welcome` — Open on a clean route and the lesson title.
2. `ct-something-shifted` — Several possible disruptions bend one shared path.
3. `ct-name-it` — The noise clears and the learner pauses at a naming point.
4. `ct-research-horizon` — The route expands across the research markers “3–5” and “5 years.”
5. `ct-five-weeks-months` — Short false expectations compress against the route.
6. `ct-five-years` — The true five-year horizon expands across the frame.
7. `ct-permission-slip` — The long horizon folds into a literal permission slip.
8. `ct-three-tracks` — The SCLA track system moves from foundation to momentum to redirection.
9. `ct-three-things` — Three empty runway checkpoints appear.
10. `ct-internal-shift` — The first checkpoint reveals the move beneath the move.
11. `ct-experiments` — The second checkpoint becomes a cluster of small test paths.
12. `ct-runway` — The third checkpoint locks story, plan, and runway together.
13. `ct-honest-promise` — The route passes through grief, uncertainty, and doubt without stopping.
14. `ct-question` — The marker reaches a three-way junction and leaves the learner with the opening question.

## Motion character

Movement is calm, finite, and purposeful: route draws, marker advances, cards
settle, and states transform. Each beat has one clear visual change, with no
infinite loops. The final frame resolves by illuminating the junction ahead.
