# The Opportunity Constellation

This lesson turns relationship-building into a clear, human map. Three recurring
nodes—peers, sponsors, and opportunity holders—move from separate definitions
into a focused constellation around the learner's next move.

The visual language uses precise lines, labeled cards, and small groups of
people rather than generic networking imagery. The story progresses from a wide
field of possible contacts to a deliberate map of three to five relationships.
The closing frame leaves the learner with one simple question and a visible
first connection.
