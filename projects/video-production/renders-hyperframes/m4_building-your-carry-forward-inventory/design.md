# Design — The Carry-Forward Inventory Case

## Concept

The lesson takes place inside one persistent inventory case. Four fixed drawers hold the portable assets from a prior career: Skills and Capabilities, Values and Drivers, Relationships and Network, and Insights and Perspective. The case stays on screen across all 29 sentence beats while drawers activate, evidence signals accumulate, and a gold carry-forward path advances.

## Persistent Carrier

- One fixed inventory case with four labeled drawers.
- A fixed portability filter: old context stays; genuine assets travel.
- Twenty-nine progressive asset signals show the inventory becoming explicit.
- A fixed forward-path rail connects the inventory to the next chapter.
- A right-side insight rail delivers one concise idea per narrated sentence.

## Visual System

- 1920 × 1080 deep-navy field with a white insight rail.
- Gold marks the selected portable asset and forward direction.
- Blue organizes categories and structural hierarchy.
- Local Proxima Nova faces and a pinned local GSAP runtime only.
- Header and progress elements remain inside declared safe regions.

## Motion

- One paused, seek-safe GSAP timeline.
- Every sentence insight appears within one second.
- The active drawer and matching inventory handle illuminate without replacing the case.
- A slow field pulse and progress line maintain continuity between beats.
- Motion is deterministic at arbitrary seek times, with no timers or infinite loops.
