# Design: Building Your Carry-Forward Inventory

## Frame system

- Canvas: 1920 × 1080.
- Safe frame: 120 px on the left and right; all lesson content ends above 960 px.
- Persistent furniture: small `CAREER TRANSITIONS` label, progress rail, and lesson title.
- Primary stage: a wide four-drawer inventory case built from flat cards and ruled lines.

## Palette

- Navy `#0d2437` and deep navy `#0a1e2f` for structure and the old context.
- Paper `#ffffff` and cultured `#f6f6f9` for inventory surfaces.
- Blue `#3393d6` for organization and category boundaries.
- Gold `#eaab2d` for chosen, portable assets and the final forward path.
- Ink `#292f35` for readable body copy.

## Type

Use Proxima Nova with the system fallback. Headlines are 72–104 px at weight 900. Supporting statements are 40–48 px. Labels are 22–26 px, uppercase, weight 700, with generous tracking. Keep all meaningful type at or above the declared minimum.

## Motion

The visual system behaves like a working inventory: rules draw, cards sort, drawers open, and selected assets settle into place. Entrances finish within 1.2 seconds. Each scene has a clear still-reading interval. Transitions move horizontally toward the future. The final resolve joins all four category colors into one gold forward line. No infinite animation.

## Accessibility

High contrast is maintained on every surface. Color never carries category meaning alone; every item includes a label and number. Text remains concise and does not reproduce full narration on screen.
