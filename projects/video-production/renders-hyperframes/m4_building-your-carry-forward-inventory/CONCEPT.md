# Carry Forward: The Inventory Table

## Core idea

Turn a career transition into a visible act of packing, not erasing. A single inventory table grows across four clearly labeled drawers: capabilities, values, relationships, and perspective. Each drawer receives a small set of plain-language asset cards, then the complete inventory travels forward into a bright next chapter.

## Visual story

1. Begin with one decisive question and an empty carry-forward case.
2. Establish the difference between what belongs to an old role and what genuinely travels.
3. Build the four-category inventory one drawer at a time.
4. Reassemble all four categories into one complete asset count.
5. Move the filled case across a threshold to show that the learner is not starting from zero.

## Direction

Use editorial diagrams, inventory cards, ruled lines, and restrained object motion. Keep the composition human and practical rather than corporate. Gold identifies what is actively selected to carry; blue organizes categories; navy anchors the old context. The final frame should feel earned: a visibly full inventory moving forward.
