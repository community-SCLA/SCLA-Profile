# Career Route: From Starting Point to First Commitment

## Visual thesis

Early Career Boost is a guided route, not a catalog of lessons. A single gold route line begins at **START HERE**, moves through the program’s milestones, and resolves at the learner’s first submitted commitment. Each scene advances the same journey so progress is visible, cumulative, and active.

## Recurring carrier

A continuous route line with numbered milestone nodes. The route changes form as the lesson progresses: horizontal orientation path, progress rail, activity loop, question pathway, mindset switch, practical-skills route, and finally a handwritten-style commitment line that locks into a completed first milestone.

## Beat progression

1. **Starting point** — Early Career Boost appears as the first coordinate on a wider career journey.
2. **Build your foundation** — the route extends across a milestone rail; checkmarks and a progress fill show where the learner is and what comes next.
3. **Learn by doing** — the route moves through click-through activity, reflection, upload, Career Mapping Tool, and one-click support.
4. **Mindset first** — a limiting-belief barrier opens and the route changes from a closed loop into a forward growth path.
5. **Questions that move you** — the path branches through dream job, AI-era skills, networking, résumé, and opportunity search, then reconverges.
6. **Make the commitment** — Workbook and AI-Career Commitment routes merge into one short statement.
7. **Submit and move** — the statement becomes the first checked milestone; the route continues beyond frame toward the learner’s goals.

## Three milestone frames

- **Orientation frame:** START HERE node at left, the Early Career Boost route crossing the canvas, and a faint continuation toward the broader journey.
- **Learning frame:** a live progress rail anchors a sequence of doing-based actions, with help visibly connected rather than isolated.
- **Resolution frame:** the learner’s commitment statement clicks into milestone one; the completed node energizes the route ahead.

## Motion logic

The line draws only forward. Nodes activate in narration order with short transform/opacity entrances; completed steps switch from blue/navy to gold. Scene exits remove supporting copy while preserving a transformed echo of the route, so the carrier feels continuous. Each scene includes one final-second resolve: a check, connection, merge, or forward extension.

## Primary risk

The route could become diagram-heavy. Limit each beat to one dominant route transformation, use large human-readable labels, and keep supporting copy to short phrases taken directly from the narration.
