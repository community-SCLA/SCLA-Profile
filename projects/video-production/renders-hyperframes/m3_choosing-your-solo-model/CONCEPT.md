# Choosing Your Solo Model

## Core idea

Turn the choice among freelance, consulting, and fractional work into a clear visual comparison. Each model receives its own working metaphor—projects, a diagnostic lens, and an embedded leadership seat—before all three resolve into a simple path from easiest first test to a possible next step.

## Visual arc

1. Open on the shift from **what you offer** to **how you work**.
2. Introduce three distinct model cards.
3. Show freelancing as a finite project moving from brief to deliverable.
4. Show consulting as diagnosis becoming insight and a plan.
5. Show fractional work as a leader embedded in a team over time.
6. Compress the lesson into three memorable shorthand statements.
7. Map a practical progression from freelance to consulting or fractional work.
8. End with an active choice: starting point and possible next direction.

## Tone

Clear, encouraging, and practical. The composition should feel like a useful decision tool, not a ranking. Gold marks the active choice, blue carries structure, and generous white space keeps the comparison calm.
