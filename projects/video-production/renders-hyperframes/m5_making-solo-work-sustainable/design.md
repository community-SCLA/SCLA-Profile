# Design — Making Solo Work Sustainable

## Direction

**Calm operating loop.** Editorial diagrams and tactile work cards make business systems feel manageable rather than corporate. The composition moves from scattered fragments to a balanced loop.

## Palette and type

- Deep navy (`#0d2437`) anchors every frame.
- Paper (`#ffffff`) and cultured white (`#f6f6f9`) carry the working objects.
- SCLA blue (`#3393d6`) connects the system.
- Gold (`#eaab2d`) marks active choices and boundaries.
- Proxima Nova, with the supplied system fallback, is used at 40px or larger for body copy.

## Layout

- 120px frame padding and a 120px footer reserve.
- Persistent top-left module label and bottom progress rail.
- One primary teaching diagram per beat; no dashboard clutter.
- Cards use 12px corners, strong spacing, and short labels.

## Motion

- Elements settle within 1.2 seconds using `power3.out`.
- Cards enter with 12–18px translation and opacity; routes draw left to right.
- The outgoing content layer clears in 0.3 seconds.
- The final second adds a gold guardrail resolve.
- No infinite repeats or non-deterministic effects.

## Accessibility

High-contrast text, large type, semantic narration text, and information that does not depend on color alone.
