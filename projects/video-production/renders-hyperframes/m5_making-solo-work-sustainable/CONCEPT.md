# Making Solo Work Sustainable

## Core idea

Turn an overwhelming solo-business workload into a calm, visible system. A single flowing “operating loop” accumulates five practical supports—tracking, intake, templates, review, and boundaries—before resolving into a sustainable weekly plan.

## Visual story

The lesson begins with scattered work orbiting one founder. Each teaching beat replaces disorder with one clear, useful object. A blue route connects those objects while gold marks the active decision. The final frame gathers the system into a balanced time budget with guardrails on both sides.

## Beat plan

1. **No system:** scattered tasks show why good work can still feel stressful.
2. **Keep it simple:** five empty system nodes replace a complex dashboard.
3. **Track opportunities:** a compact pipeline moves contacts through clear stages.
4. **Create intake:** a yes triggers a short, dependable handoff path.
5. **Reuse templates:** repeatable documents stack into a practical toolkit.
6. **Review weekly:** a one-hour review closes the loop before work becomes reactive.
7. **Set boundaries:** time, scope, client, and timeline guardrails protect the system.
8. **Choose sustainability:** a time budget and selected systems form the action plan.

## Motion approach

One paused GSAP timeline drives every state. Scenes use short, eased entrances, restrained route drawing, and additive final resolves. All motion is deterministic and seek-safe; no infinite animation is used.
