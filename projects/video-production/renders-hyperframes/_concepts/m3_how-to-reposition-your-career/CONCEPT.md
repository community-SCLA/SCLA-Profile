# CONCEPT — m3_how-to-reposition-your-career

Judged against the two APPROVED rows in `design-system/docs/taste.md` only.
Runtime basis: 531 narration words → **≈3:40 (≈220s)** at the pinned voice.
Quarter marks by word position: **133 / 266 / 398 / 531** — inside Alex's three
moves, on Jordan's "The classroom didn't disappear," inside the four-step
pattern at "Curate your story," and on the close. Four different zones of the
script, so four different pictures are available. Percentages are authoritative;
clock times are approximate.

---

## Concept angle

**Three careers drawn as three upright core samples standing in a field rack —
strata already laid down in the order they happened, which nobody can re-stack —
that gain tags, name cards and finally four rules read straight across all
three.** It carries every frame after the title card, from the first content
beat (≈0:08) to the final beat: **≈95% of runtime**, against a 60% floor.

## The carrying object

**A core sample standing upright in a steel cradle**, drawn flat-vector in brand
tones — no photo-realism, no glass, no light source.

- **The column.** One upright rectangle, roughly 55% of frame height and stout
  (never tall-and-thin), filled edge to edge, top to bottom, with **horizontal
  strata bands of irregular thickness** — no two neighbouring bands the same
  tone, at least one band visibly **tilted** across the bank. A column arrives
  **complete**: every band is already there the moment it enters, and no band is
  ever added, removed, re-toned, re-sized or re-ordered afterwards. This is the
  script's guardrail — *you're not erasing your past* — made physically
  impossible to violate.
- **The apparatus.** Each column sits in a simple two-armed cradle; all cradles
  share one visible baseline shelf. A **collar cap** runs across the top of each
  column carrying that person's role in the script's own words. Small,
  **unnumbered** depth ticks run up the left edge (a number would be a fact the
  script does not make).
- **Tags.** Small brand-accent flags on a short stem, clipped to the right edge
  of one individual band. A tag is **blank** when clipped and **lettered** when
  the narration names it. Once clipped, a tag never moves and never un-clips.
- **Name cards.** Clean white rectangles standing to the right of the bank, one
  per person. **All readable copy in the video lives on the cards** — headings
  Title Case with no terminal period, the quoted bullets and summaries in the
  script's own sentence case. The strata carry shape only and are never asked to
  hold small type.
- **The capped blank tube.** In the opening minute, one sealed, capped, entirely
  **unbanded** tube is set down flat along the bottom-left — the "starting
  completely over" picture: new degree, new industry, new identity. It is set
  down once, never opened, and stays visible and blank for the rest of the
  video.
- **Correlation rules.** In the last quarter only, four horizontal brand-accent
  bands draw left to right **across all three columns at once**, each landing on
  a band already tagged in every column, each ending in a label chip at the right
  margin. They are **unevenly spaced**, because they follow real strata — a grid
  is evenly spaced, and this must never read as one.

## Milestone frames — the builder's ACCUMULATION CONTRACT

**25% (≈0:55, inside Alex's three moves).** ONE column stands in its cradle in
the left third, fully banded from arrival, four tags clipped to four separate
bands — three still blank, one lettered. One name card stands to the right at
half the column's height carrying the rewritten bullet in full: "Led a
multi-department initiative that reduced processing time by 25% and improved
customer satisfaction." The capped blank tube lies closed along the bottom-left.
*Composition: a single vertical mass at left, one small card at right, a wide
belt of open canvas between them.* Reads as **one record being read**.

**50% (≈1:50, "The classroom didn't disappear.").** TWO columns stand side by
side. The second gains **no band and loses none** — its strata are exactly as
they arrived — while its collar cap is struck through and re-lettered to
"Learning Experience Designer" and its card is written: "Designed and
implemented multi-modal learning experiences for diverse learners." The card
stack has grown to hold the right half of the canvas, and for the first time a
short **leader line** runs from a tagged band across to its card. *Composition:
two registers of roughly equal weight — a bank at left, a stack at right —
physically linked.* Reads as **a record being turned into something other people
hold**.

**75% (≈2:45, inside the four-step pattern at "Curate your story").** THREE
columns fill the width edge to edge, all tagged, Priya's tags carrying "Mentored
3 new analysts, accelerating their ramp-up time by 30%." The cards compress into
a narrow strip along the right margin, and the **first two of four correlation
rules** are drawing horizontally across all three columns at once. *Composition:
a dense filled rectangle crossed by horizontals — the busiest frame in the
video.* Reads as **three separate records about to be shown as one procedure**.

**100% (≈3:40, the close).** Inverted and top-weighted. ONE card, enlarged, is
the biggest object in the video and holds the upper half of the canvas — where
at 0:55 the biggest object was a column. Beneath it the three columns are drawn
as a short compressed bank, all four rules complete and labelled. Along the
bottom rail the same card sits in the four formats the script names: resume,
LinkedIn headline, summary, and 60-second career story. The capped blank tube is
still there, still unopened. *Composition: nothing vertical dominates; the frame
is top-heavy over a compressed base.* Reads as **the thing other people see,
standing on a record that never changed**.

Four pictures: one mass in air → a balanced linked split → a saturated width
crossed by horizontals → a top-weighted enlargement over a compressed bank.

## What accumulates

Countable off the picture without reading a word:

1. **Columns:** 1 → 2 → 3. The bank saturates at three; there is no room for a
   fourth.
2. **Tags clipped to bands:** monotone increase, never un-clipped, never moved.
3. **Lettering on tags and collar caps:** blank → struck → set. One-way.
4. **Name cards:** 0 → 1 → 2 → 3, then one of them enlarged.
5. **Correlation rules across all three columns:** 0 → 4, last quarter only.
6. **Formats along the bottom rail:** 0 → 4, final beat only.

And the one thing that never changes, which is the point: **the strata**. Not a
band added, removed, re-ordered or re-toned for the whole runtime. Every gain is
a reading, a label, or a connection — never new material.

## The payoff beat

**≈3:15–3:25, on "revealing the through-line in what you've already done."** The
enlarged card is laid back flat over the three columns, and every line on it
lands on a band that was already drawn and already tagged before 1:00 — the
through-line is literally the four rules, now visible at once as one horizontal
reading across three separate careers. *You cannot log a unit that is not in the
core*: the picture forbids the failure the script warns about, because no
unearned title can appear where there is no band. Undrawable at 0:10 — at 0:10
the rack is empty and the only object on screen is a capped, blank, unopened
tube.

## Grafted from the runner-up

Pitch A's strongest move, kept: **the object annotates itself as a method in the
last quarter.** The four correlation rules take their label chips from four
structural parts of the apparatus — the collar cap (clarify direction), the tags
(curate), the name cards (translate), the bottom rail of formats (update) — so
the closing quarter re-reads the apparatus itself as the four-step pattern
rather than just showing a fuller container. Also grafted: A's explicit
anti-addition clause at 50% ("nothing moves shelves; only the cards change"),
bound here as the hard builder rule that Jordan's column gains no band and loses
none while its cap and card are re-written.

## Batch-diversity note

**Re-cast performed.** Pitch B won on accumulation logic but was cast as a
**darkroom light table with negative strips and a drying line** — assigned to a
sibling M3 lesson and therefore unavailable. B's ladder is preserved intact —
a fixed, un-reorderable record that arrives complete; readings marked onto it;
one public output per record; a band drawn across all records at once; one
output enlarged over the unchanged records — and re-cast onto **upright core
samples in a steel field rack**.

How this differs from the siblings' assigned carriers: the **light table** is a
lit horizontal bed of translucent film seen through glass; the **gallery wall**
is a vertical plane of framed works hung on a rail; the **lighthouse lens** is a
rotating optic throwing a beam outward. This carrier has no glass, no light
source, no frames, no rail and no beam — it is opaque mineral apparatus standing
in a cradle on a baseline, and its payoff is a horizontal correlation read across
separate upright columns, a picture none of those three can produce. It is also
explicitly none of: a lit museum or exhibit case (no glass, no shelves, no
holding shelf), a brick wall or skyline (see the guard below), a ruled paper
worksheet, a peg-and-thread board, a scrap field feeding a statement strip, a
trail marker, a career track with a checkpoint node, plant cuttings, a potted
tree on wires, or a truss span.

## Judge's note

**Likeliest failure mode: three tall banded rectangles rendering as a bar chart
or a skyline** — the moment that happens the video is dataviz, not illustration,
and the carrier stops depicting anything.

**Concrete guard:** all three columns are the **same height and the same width**
(a chart varies height; a skyline varies both), stout rather than tall-and-thin,
seated in a **visible steel cradle on one shared baseline shelf**, capped by a
**collar** across the top, with unnumbered depth ticks up the left edge. Bands
run **edge to edge at irregular thicknesses** with at least one band **tilted**
across the bank — no bar chart and no building has a dip. The correlation rules
are unevenly spaced because they follow real strata. Test at snapshot review: if
a still could be captioned as a chart, the bands are too regular — thicken and
de-regularise them before anything else.

**Corollary guard (motion):** settled strata never re-animate. The only motion in
the video is a tag clipping on, a card being written, or a rule drawing left to
right. The 100% re-composition is drawn once at a scene boundary — the bank is
*presented* smaller in that scene, never shrunk in place — so nothing already
settled is re-animated.

## Scores

**Pitch A — the lit museum exhibit case.** It names a genuinely concrete object
and its accumulation is real and countable (lit objects, written cards,
lettering on the title strip), and its 50% anti-addition clause — the objects do
not move shelves, only their labels change — is the sharpest single reading of
the lesson in either pitch. But it fails the four-frame test, which grades
pictures and not verbs: SELECT, RELABEL and SURFACE-FROM-THE-BACK are three
variations on the same picture, a case seen front-on with a different shelf
active, and the payoff is a **camera move** — crossing to the corridor side of
the glass — that flat brand vector cannot actually show, so the closing frame
risks being the 75% frame with a placard added. That is the thin-cut shape:
state changes a viewer reads by re-reading copy rather than by re-reading the
picture. **Verdict: RUNNER-UP** — its self-annotation idea is grafted above.

**Pitch B — the darkroom light table.** Equally concrete on the object, and the
only pitch that made **composition** rather than content the differentiator: one
horizontal band → two registers → a saturated bed → a top-weighted enlargement.
It is the only pitch that did the runtime arithmetic and placed its quarter
marks against real script positions, and re-checking those positions confirms
them (133 / 266 / 398 / 531 land in four different zones of the script). Its
accumulation is countable off the picture with no copy read at all, removal is
made physically impossible rather than merely discouraged, and its payoff —
*you cannot print what is not on the negative* — is the script's own guardrail
drawn rather than narrated. It closes the lazy-build hole with five prop state
changes no heading swap can fake. **Verdict: WINNER**, re-cast onto core samples
per the batch-diversity constraint; the ladder, the anti-removal logic and the
composition arc carry over unchanged.
