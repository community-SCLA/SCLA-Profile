# PITCH B — accumulation-first

**Stem:** `finding-creating-a-career-purpose-statement_early-career-boost`
**Lens:** accumulation-first — the build-up was designed before the object was named.

**Runtime basis.** The script is 363 narrated words across 7 paragraphs. At the
pinned voice's working rate that lands around **2:30 (~150s)**. Paragraph ends
fall at roughly **15 / 38 / 59 / 80 / 103 / 131 / 148s**. Every mark below is
stated as a fraction of runtime as well as a second, so the ladder re-scales
cleanly if the synthesized wav comes in longer or shorter.

---

## The accumulation ladder

Designed first, before any object was named: what is physically MORE, denser,
re-sorted, or newly connected in the frame at each mark.

**By ~30s (20% — "one or two sentences… not permanent… a working answer")**
The frame has gained **raw material and an empty measured container**. A field
of small blank marks has drifted in (the work already done — the script's
"criteria, energy themes, mapped options", drawn as three tints), and above it a
single empty two-line slot has been ruled. The slot has also gained its
*measure*: a crop bracket clamps it to two lines and a hard right edge, its
border is drawn in dashed pencil weight rather than solid ink, and a small
question tag now hangs beneath it. Gained: ~two dozen marks, one ruled slot,
one crop bracket, one tag. Nothing yet is filled.

**By ~60s (40% — "blends three things")**
The frame has gained **order without content**. The drifted marks have migrated
into **three vertical columns**, each parked directly beneath one third of the
slot, each column gaining a left-edge label from the script's own three
sources. The slot itself gains three faint division ticks so the columns and
the slot line up. Gained: a re-sort (the same marks, now grouped and aligned) +
three labels + three ticks. The container is still empty — that tension is the
point.

**By ~90s (60% — "I want to use these strengths… direction, contribution, and a reason")**
The frame has gained **its first readable content, and its first exclusions**.
One mark rises out of each column into its slot and **enlarges into legible
copy**, and the connective words are inked between them, so the slot now reads
as one continuous sentence. The three columns are visibly *lighter* — each has
a hole where its promoted mark left. Outside the crop bracket, in the margin,
**two struck-through tags accumulate** ("grand claims", "buzzwords") — the
things the script says are not in there, kept on the board as evidence rather
than deleted. Then the same three filled slots gain a **second label row**
underneath: direction / contribution / a reason. Gained: three filled slots,
three column holes, two margin tags, one re-read of content already on screen.

**By ~120s (80% — the qualities, then "write three quick versions")**
The frame has gained **a loaded rail, and the object gets acted on**. Five small
chips clip one at a time along the slot's **lower edge**, each carrying one
quality the script names. On the last chip — "brief" — the slot's right edge
**pulls in one notch**, a single declared move: the object it is describing
physically tightens. Then the slot gains **three rough carbon copies** stacked
below it, same skeleton, different marks pulled from the columns, so the
columns lose three more marks each. Gained: 5 chips, 1 dimensional change to
the object itself, 3 draft copies, 9 more column holes.

**By ~150s (100% — "a method you can return to, and a purpose to point it at")**
The frame has gained **a decision and a circuit**. One draft is circled; the
circled one lifts to a **mount bracket at the head of the board**; and a closed
**loop arrow** is ruled beneath the whole board with its arrowhead landing on
the mounted line. Nothing from the run has been cleared: the emptied columns,
the crop bracket, the margin tags, the five chips, the two unchosen drafts and
the mounted line are all on screen at once. Gained: 1 selection mark, 1 mount,
1 closed loop, and the whole history readable in a single still.

## The carrying object

**A blank ruled statement strip, fed by a field of note-scraps that re-sorts
into three columns and then empties upward into the strip's three slots.**

*How it is drawn.* The **strip** is one wide rounded rectangle across the upper
third of the canvas — a 3px dashed brand-blue outline, two hairline rules
inside at the one-third and two-thirds heights (that is "one or two sentences",
drawn as capacity), three faint vertical ticks dividing it into slots, and a
square crop bracket clamping its right edge. The **scraps** are ~24 small
rounded rects, roughly 90×56px, in three brand tints, each rotated a few
degrees off square and carrying two short grey graphic rules inside — they hold
**no type at all**, so nothing on the board sits under the 40px text floor until
a scrap is promoted into a slot and enlarges into real, legible copy. The
**chips** are 5 small pill shapes clipped to the strip's lower rail; the
**drafts** are three flatter, rougher repeats of the strip below it; the
**mount** is a plain bracket with one up-tick, and the **loop** is a single
closed rounded-rect arrow ruled around the board's lower margin.

*Scrap count is texture, never a claim.* The field is never counted, labeled,
or referred to on-frame — same move as the approved 48-mark reference cut.

## Four frames

**25% (~0:37) — Full mess, empty container.**
A dashed two-line strip is ruled across the top with its crop bracket and a
question tag hanging under it. Below, ~24 blank scraps sit in an unsorted
drift, tilted, overlapping, spread across the lower two-thirds. Nothing is
aligned to anything. The strip is conspicuously, deliberately empty.

**50% (~1:15) — Order below, still empty above.**
The drift is gone as a drift: the same scraps now stand in three tight vertical
columns, edges aligned, each column labeled at its left and parked under one
slot of the strip. The strip has gained three division ticks. The picture is
now a grid feeding a container — and the container has still not been filled.
Same marks as frame 1, completely different geometry.

**75% (~1:52) — Filled, labeled twice, and hung with chips.**
The strip carries one enlarged, legible scrap in each slot with the connective
words inked between them, plus a second label row beneath reading direction /
contribution / a reason. Four or five chips are clipped along its lower rail.
The columns below are visibly gap-toothed where marks have left. Two
struck-through tags sit in the right margin, outside the crop bracket. Dense at
the top, thinning at the bottom — the inverse of frame 1.

**100% (~2:30) — The whole board at once.**
One line is mounted at the head of the board under a bracket. Three drafts sit
below the strip's old position, one of them circled. A closed loop arrow runs
around the lower margin with its arrowhead touching the mounted line. The
columns are sparse and holed. Five chips, two margin tags, one crop bracket all
still present. The eye can read the entire run bottom-to-top: scraps → columns
→ strip → drafts → the one line, with the loop pointing back at it.

Four genuinely different pictures: (1) scatter under an empty slot, (2) grid
under an empty slot, (3) filled slot over a thinning grid, (4) mounted line over
the whole spent board with a circuit closed.

## The payoff beat

**The final beat (para 7, ~2:12–2:30): the mount and the closed loop.**

Two things resolve on the same beat. First the object **completes** — the strip
that was ruled empty at 0:20 is now filled, chosen, tightened, and lifted onto
a mount at the head of the frame; the loop's arrowhead lands on it, which is the
script's own "revise it every single time you run your decision loop" and "a
purpose, in your own words, to point it at" drawn as one connection. Second the
board **re-reads**: with the loop closed, the emptied columns and the drafts
stop being leftovers and become the visible method — the thing the script says
confidence actually comes from. This closing frame is not drawable at 0:10,
where the board holds one empty dashed rectangle and two dozen wordless marks.

A second, smaller re-read happens mid-run at ~0:80: the three filled slots gain
their direction / contribution / reason labels without any new content
entering — the same three objects, read a second way.

## Why this is not lazy

A builder cannot satisfy this with heading swaps against a static prop, because
**the prop's geometry is different in every milestone window and the differences
are countable**:

- **Scrap positions change three times and never return.** Drift → three
  aligned columns → columns with three holes → columns with twelve holes. Two
  contact sheets can always be ordered by counting marks per column, which is
  exactly the shuffle test. A static prop with new headings produces identical
  scrap geometry across every sheet and fails that test instantly.
- **The container's interior state has four distinct values.** Empty → ticked
  into three slots → three slots filled with enlarged legible copy → filled plus
  a second label row. None of these is a heading swap; each is a structural
  change inside the same shape.
- **The object's own dimensions change once, on cue.** The right edge pulls in
  a notch at the word "brief". A prop that never moves cannot fake that.
- **Things attach and stay attached.** Five chips on the lower rail and two
  struck tags in the margin arrive one at a time and are never removed, so any
  frame's chip count and tag count date it.
- **The object relocates on the last beat.** The strip leaves the upper third
  and moves onto a mount, and a loop is drawn that did not exist before —
  changing the canvas composition itself, not its labels.

**Milestone contract for `CONCEPT.md` (falsifiable, gradeable off snapshots):**
at ~0:30 the strip is empty and the field is unsorted; at ~1:00 the field is in
three aligned columns and the strip is still empty; at ~1:30 all three slots
carry enlarged legible copy and each column shows one hole; at ~2:00 five chips
are on the rail, the right edge has tightened, and three drafts exist; at close
one draft is circled and mounted with a closed loop pointing at it. Any build
that cannot show those five states in its snapshot grid has not built this
concept.

**Motion discipline.** Every gain above is a single one-time entrance or move,
after which the element is pixel-static — no wobble, drift, pulse, or re-mark on
settled content. The one dimensional change (the right-edge notch) is a declared
single move, not a keep-alive.

**Fidelity note.** Everything drawn is a re-reading of the narration's own
words: the three prior artifacts, "one or two sentences", "not permanent", the
working question, the three things it blends, the structure sentence's three
parts, "no grand claims, no buzzwords", "direction, contribution, and a reason",
the five named qualities, "three quick versions", and "your decision loop". No
count, fact, or sequence claim is introduced that the script does not say — the
five chips must be exactly the five qualities named, with no synthesized sixth,
and the scrap field is never counted on-frame.
