# PITCH B — m4_visibility-actions

Lens: **accumulation-first.** The gain was designed before the object was named.
Script is ~300 words at the pinned voice, so assume **≈125s runtime**; the marks
below are 30 / 60 / 90 / 120s and scale proportionally if the built cut differs.

---

## The accumulation ladder

What the frame must have *gained*, decided before anything was named:

**By 30s — a measure, and the first few units inside it.**
The frame starts as an almost-empty channel with a hard left wall and a set
width. By 0:30 it holds three or four small identical units, seated against
that wall, plus the wall itself: a boundary that says how wide this line is
allowed to be. Gain = *a container, and a little in it.* Nothing is grouped
yet, and the empty run of channel to the right is the visible promise.

**By 60s — population and density.**
The single densest moment of the video. A compartmented store above the
channel is open, and five distinguishable units now stand proud on the tray
below it, each carrying a different small mark on its face. The first line in
the channel is full to the wall. Gain = *many, and visibly different from one
another* — you can no longer count them at a glance, which is the point of the
"examples" stretch of the narration.

**By 90s — re-sort into rigid structure.**
Loose population collapses into arrangement. Three lines are lifted out of the
channel and squared inside a rectangular frame; blank spacer blocks fill the
air around them and two wedges tighten at one corner until the whole block is
solid enough to pick up in one hand. Gain = *the same metal, now load-bearing.*
Whitespace stops being emptiness and becomes fitted parts. Almost none of the
loose units from 60s remain loose.

**By 120s — a transfer of kind.**
The block does not change again; something new appears beside it, on a
material that was not in the frame before. Ink, pressure, and a printed
impression that reads forward while the metal it came from still reads
backwards. Gain = *a second surface that anyone can read without knowing
anything about the first.* The whole 120s of small placements is now visible in
one glance, in one image, at one time.

Note the ladder's shape: **count → difference → structure → substrate.** Each
rung gains in a different currency, so no two stretches of the video are doing
the same kind of work.

## The carrying object

**A composing stick of hand-set metal type — the tray of loose sorts, the
compartmented case above it, and the rectangular chase they get locked into —
which at the end prints a single legible proof.**

It is drawn in flat SCLA-brand vector: each sort is a plain tall rectangle with
a shoulder line and a notch (the nick) cut into its top edge, so a row of them
reads as manufactured units rather than as bars on a chart; the composing stick
is a shallow L-channel in a heavier weight with one hard wall and a sliding
knee that sets the measure. The case is a grid of open compartments at the top
of the frame, deep enough to hold a shadowed interior. In the last act the
chase is a plain rectangle in the heaviest stroke on screen, spacer blocks are
flat unstroked fills, and the printed proof is the only object in the whole
video made of ink on white rather than line on ground — so the payoff is a
material change, not a color change.

## Four frames

**25% (~31s) — "the empty measure."**
Camera low and tight, sighted along the channel of the composing stick. The
hard left wall is at frame-left; four blank sorts are seated against it; the
rest of the channel runs out to the right, empty and clean. The case above is
closed. Dominant read: *one long empty groove with a little metal in it.*
Colour: line on ground, high air.

**50% (~62s) — "the case is open."**
Camera overhead and wide. The compartment grid fills the top third, open, its
cells visibly unequal. On the tray below, five sorts stand face-up and
separated, each bearing a different small device — a raised platform mark, a
short broadcast bar, a hooked question mark, a stepped-up bar, a pair of linked
marks. The stick's first line is full to the wall. Dominant read: *crowded,
varied, the busiest picture in the video.* Nothing here is centered; it reads
as a working surface.

**75% (~93s) — "locked up."**
Camera three-quarter, mid distance. A heavy rectangular chase now dominates
frame-center. Inside it: three tight lines of type, and around them fitted
spacer blocks in flat fill with two wedges biting at the top-right corner. The
tray is bare — the population went somewhere. One of the three lines sits a
hair proud of the other two. Dominant read: *a solid rectangle you could lift,
made of the things that were loose a minute ago.*

**100% (~125s) — "the pull."**
Camera flat-on to a sheet. The chase has slid to the frame edge, simplified and
quiet; the sheet holds a crisp forward-reading impression at large size,
directly beneath the mirrored metal it came from. A second, identical
impression sits under the first, offset a few millimetres. One small patch on
the top proof inked light, and a hand-drawn ring marks it. Dominant read: *ink
on paper — a different material than every previous frame.*

Four different camera distances, four different dominant materials, four
different compositions. No two of these could be confused in a shuffled contact
sheet.

---

## The payoff beat

**The pull, on the closing line** — "easier to see, and easier for others to
support."

The metal has been legible only to the person setting it, and only backwards,
for the entire runtime. At the payoff it transfers: the same arrangement,
unchanged, appears forward-reading on a surface anyone can pick up. Every sort
placed since 0:10 is present in that single impression at once — the closing
frame is a *re-read of the whole build*, not a new picture. The second offset
impression under the first is the narration's own "consistent reputation over
time" (the same forme, pulled again, in register); the light patch with the
ring drawn round it is the narration's own "isn't about being perfect… reflect
on what you learn."

Script fidelity: five distinguishable example sorts because the narration names
five kinds of visibility action; three lines locked into the chase because the
narration says design three; one line standing proud and inked first because
the narration says choose one to test in the next 30 days. No count, order, or
claim appears that the narration does not speak.

---

## Why this is not lazy

**It fails the heading-swap test by construction.** A builder cannot hold one
prop still and change titles over it: the stick's fill level, the open case,
the lock-up geometry and the printed sheet are four different objects made of
three different materials at three different camera distances. There is no
static prop to swap headings against — the milestone frames above are the
contract.

**It accumulates in the approved direction, not the rejected one.** Every beat
*adds* one thing — one sort, one spacer, one wedge, one turn of the roller —
and nothing already placed moves again. That is the low-churn / high-idea-rate
signature of the two APPROVED reference cuts, and it is the opposite of
answering "boring" with motion. Roughly 20 discrete additions across ~125s
lands near 10 beats/min without splitting a single idea in half, and settled
metal is pixel-static by design (no wobble to author, nothing for
`check_motion.py` to catch).

**It survives the thin-carrier test.** The rejected thin cut was six dots on an
empty frame that never gained detail. Here the object gains three separate
kinds of thing — count, then structure, then a new substrate — so the shuffle
test is trivially passable and the carrier occupies the working area of the
frame at every mark rather than sitting as a small prop beside white space.

**It is visibly different from the rest of this batch.** No bricks, sheets,
threads, plants, spans, frames, glass or cores. The nearest structural neighbour
in the batch is "a scrap field feeding a statement strip," and this diverges on
all three acts: the units are drawn from a compartmented inventory rather than
found as scrap; the middle act is a mechanical lock-up with fitted spacers and
wedges rather than a strip being fed; and the payoff is a transfer onto a
material that has not appeared before, which a field-to-strip concept cannot
do. On screen the two look nothing alike.

**The metaphor is the lesson's own argument, not decoration.** Type is small,
silent and set one piece at a time — the narration's "none of these require you
to be loud." A single sort means nothing; the arrangement is the meaning — the
narration's "link your name to meaningful contributions." And metal reads
backwards until it is put where others can see it, which is the whole lesson in
one physical fact.
