# Design — The Opening Map

## Chosen concept

AI does not erase a role in one stroke. It compresses some tasks while higher-value human work gains space. One evolving work map makes that reallocation visible from the anxious opening question to the final career path.

## Visual carrier

The carrying object is one persistent 28-stop career route. It is built once and remains on screen for the full lesson. Every narration beat advances the same gold marker by one stop while the route, phase labels, background, header, and journey line stay fixed. The concise statement above the route changes, but the map is never discarded or redrawn.

Blue represents the changing work system; gold marks the viewer's current opening. Navy is the stable work context. The route is grouped into five persistent phases: Reframe, Audit, Move to Value, Human + AI, and Openings.

## Beat-to-frame map

- **s01–s03 — The threat frame:** Introduce the lesson and hold the common question over an intact ROLE field.
- **s04–s09 — Reframe the role:** Accept the concern, split the role into tasks, then replace the binary replacement question with a wide opportunity opening.
- **s10–s14 — Audit any role:** Run the same field through three moves: identify faster/cheaper tasks, reveal rising human value, and reposition toward it. Resolve on “AI-era edge.”
- **s15–s18 — Drafting example:** Transform the map into a document first pass. Add human layers for purpose, judgment, and audience relationship; finish by moving the career marker to those layers.
- **s19–s22 — Adapt around durable skills:** Contrast a fast AI current with six connected human skill nodes. The network remains stable while the current passes underneath.
- **s23–s25 — Work with the tools:** Reject a head-to-head contest. Curl the carrier into HUMAN QUESTION → AI DRAFT → HUMAN REVIEW → BETTER RESULT and close the circuit.
- **s26–s28 — Choose the opening:** Straighten the loop into a forward route through judgment, range, and tool fluency. Open several gold apertures and resolve on “Curiosity beats dread.”

## Milestone frames

1. **The role is not the task:** One ROLE field fractures into multiple task strips while its outer boundary remains intact.
2. **Value moves:** A blue first draft compresses to one layer while purpose, judgment, and audience trust expand above it in gold.
3. **Better together:** The four-stage collaboration circuit closes and produces a visibly larger result than either input.

## Motion logic

The route persists. At each beat, one small node changes from blue to gold and the prior node settles back to blue; the statement above the route enters and exits within the beat. A single gold journey line fills continuously from ROLE to OPENING. All motion is seek-safe on one synchronous paused GSAP timeline and uses only transform, opacity, and color.

## Frame and type

The exact 1920×1080 frame uses the workspace tokens: 120px nominal padding, 72px hard safe area, and content above y=960. Proxima Nova is vendored locally at weights 400, 700, and 900. Viewer-read sentences are at least 40px; uppercase tracked furniture is at least 20px. ## Primary risk and control

An evolving map can become abstract. Each beat therefore pairs one concise statement with a numbered stop, while five persistent phase labels keep the learner oriented from the initial reframe through the final opening.
