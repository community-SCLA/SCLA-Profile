# Future-You Resume — Concept

## What
A resume transforms from a crowded archive into a focused case file for the learner’s next move.

## Visual story
The lesson uses a single editorial desk metaphor: stacked history cards are sorted, sharpened, and connected into a clear forward path. Three warning cards identify the common problems; a north-star marker then guides evidence selection and an impact-bullet rewrite.

## Learning arc
1. Recognize the archive problem.
2. Diagnose tasks, outdated language, and a missing through-line.
3. Reframe the resume as curated evidence.
4. Use the Next Move Statement as the filter.
5. Rewrite evidence with responsibility, action, outcome, and scope.
6. Continue into the Resume Builder Tool.

## Motion
Cards enter with short vertical lifts, rules draw left-to-right, and the through-line grows across the composition. Motion is finite, restrained, and fully controlled by one seekable paused timeline.
