# Mid-Career Momentum: Your Next Move

## Creative idea

A clear career map turns a complicated mid-career moment into one practical next move. Six beats move from welcome, through orientation and choices, to a focused 90-day commitment.

## Visual approach

- Use a restrained navy, paper, blue, and gold interface inspired by a career-planning dashboard.
- Keep one central route line across the lesson, adding a new checkpoint at each beat.
- Use large, plain-language headlines and short supporting phrases rather than repeating the full narration on screen.
- End with a paper commitment card and a clear **Next** action.

## Beat plan

1. **welcome** — Establish experience and readiness for more.
2. **checkpoint** — Place the learner at a deliberate mid-career checkpoint.
3. **toolkit** — Introduce the platform activities and support path.
4. **choices** — Name the real pressures and three possible directions.
5. **next-move** — Narrow the horizon to one realistic 6–12 month move.
6. **commitment** — Capture the learner's 90-day anchor and invite them forward.
