# Rewrite Your LinkedIn for Future You

## Core idea

Turn a profile that looks backward into a clear signpost for what comes next. The lesson moves from a static, muted profile card to an active positioning system: **future role + strengths + value**.

## Visual story

An editorial LinkedIn-inspired profile canvas evolves across eleven beats. A gold “future” line travels forward, the three-part headline assembles, a before/after profile changes direction, and the About section becomes a bridge from experience to opportunity. The visual language stays human and professional rather than imitating the LinkedIn interface.

## Motion approach

One deterministic, paused GSAP timeline controls every entrance, emphasis, transition, and closing resolve. Cards slide on a restrained horizontal axis, rules draw left-to-right, and the final doorway opens with a small gold expansion.

## Accessibility

High-contrast navy, white, blue, and gold; large type; short on-screen phrases; no flashing, autoplay media, or motion that is required to understand the narration.
