# Design

## Direction

Editorial career workbook meets a clean professional profile. One cultured-paper profile canvas persists inside the deep navy frame, with blue used for structure and gold reserved for forward motion and key decisions.

## System

- Canvas: 1920 × 1080
- Type: Proxima Nova with system sans-serif fallback
- Palette: navy `#0d2437`, deep navy `#0a1e2f`, blue `#3393d6`, gold `#eaab2d`, paper `#ffffff`, cultured `#f6f6f9`
- Safe padding: 120px; bottom content edge: 960px
- Labels: uppercase, tracked, 22px minimum
- Body: 40px minimum; headings: 60–88px

## Composition

The carrying object is one persistent cultured-paper profile canvas inside the navy frame; each beat re-sorts or updates that same canvas rather than replacing the whole picture. Static biography pages stack and compress, headline and About nodes activate in sequence, the three-part headline assembles, the before/after profile shifts its emphasis forward, About themes rise into focus, and the final doorway opens wider across the closing beats. There is no playback progress rail or scene-progress indicator. On-screen copy is distilled from, never substituted for, the exact narration stored in each timed clip.

## Motion

Entrances settle within 1.2 seconds. A complete sentence or tightly connected idea enters once and stays on screen across its narration clips; internal beat boundaries never trigger a whole-canvas exit and re-entry. Meaning-bearing objects instead receive distinct, seek-safe choreography: stacked pages assemble, shift, and compress; path nodes move focus from headline to About to opportunity; formula cards build, slide, and resolve; the before/after comparison moves across its directional arrow; About themes rise in sequence; option chips sharpen in place; and the doorway opens progressively in three finite stages. The canvas exits only after the thought is complete. No infinite or full-runtime animation is used.
