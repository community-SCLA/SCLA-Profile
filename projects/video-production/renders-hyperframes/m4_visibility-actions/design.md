# Design: Visibility Actions

## Visual system

- **Canvas:** 1920 × 1080, deep navy field with generous 120px margins.
- **Palette:** navy foundation, white and cultured-paper cards, SCLA blue for structure, and gold for the active signal.
- **Type:** Proxima Nova with system fallback; large, direct headings and short supporting labels.
- **Motif:** a gold signal dot moves along a thin blue path, connecting useful contributions to the people who can support them.
- **Composition:** every beat has one focal idea. Cards and diagrams remain inside the safe area and above the footer reserve.

## Beat treatments

1. A two-part equation connects networking and visibility to momentum.
2. A crossed-out “self-promotion” card gives way to three audience cards.
3. Three verbs—place, link, build—form the definition.
4. A six-card action field presents specific examples without duplicating the narration on screen.
5. A volume dial stays low while three purposeful moments illuminate.
6. Three numbered action cards translate the module prompt into a plan.
7. One card moves into a 30-day test window.
8. A subtle path resolves from “work” to “future direction” to “support.”

## Motion and accessibility

Motion uses opacity, short vertical travel, and line drawing. Entrances settle within 1.2 seconds, exits take 0.3 seconds, and no animation repeats infinitely. On-screen text maintains high contrast and stays at or above the system type floor.
