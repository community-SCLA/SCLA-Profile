# Design — Visibility Actions

## Selected concept

**The Contribution Spotlight:** one large contribution occupies the 1920×1080 stage. Visibility actions change who can see it, what they can understand, and whether they can respond or offer support. The spotlight belongs to useful work rather than self-promotion.

## Full-stage carrier

The carrier is one persistent cause-and-effect field, not a boxed slide. Its three large regions—**Contribution → Action → Consequence**—keep the same position, size, borders, and visual weight for the entire lesson. Beat changes re-sort evidence and emphasis inside those regions; they never throw away or redraw the field:

- **Contribution:** a large work artifact, insight, question, stretch role, or teaching exchange.
- **Action:** the meaningful behavior—Present, Share, Ask, Stretch, Mentor, Offer, or Test.
- **Audience:** decision-makers, sponsors, peers, leadership, team, or community.
- **Consequence:** understanding, name association, demonstrated capability, learning, or support.

Each beat uses the entire safe frame. The contribution and audience occupy opposing regions while the action visibly changes their relationship. Negative space separates these regions; there is no unused side field.

## Hierarchy and spacing

- Persistent program and lesson title stay in the top chrome region.
- A 64–88px beat heading occupies the upper band.
- One dominant diagram fills the central 1680×560 area.
- Teaching labels are 20–28px; meaningful statements are 40–58px.
- At least 40px separates unrelated text, illustrations, audience labels, and response fields.
- No cards float over one another, and no visual element leaves the safe canvas.

## Motion logic

Motion has a named semantic role in every beat:

- **Action:** place, present, share, ask, stretch, mentor, teach, select, or test.
- **Connection:** relationship lines join named states only.
- **Reach:** a shared view opens toward a real audience.
- **Evidence:** outcomes and capability marks become readable.
- **Response:** understanding, learning, or support returns visibly.

All movement is finite, seek-safe, transform-based, and settled for reading. There are no progress bars, rails, traces, decorative dots, false controls, counters, or time-reporting motion.

## Palette and accessibility

Use only local tokens: deep navy background, navy surfaces, white and cultured teaching fields, blue structure, gold action/consequence emphasis, and muted-video secondary text. Use Proxima Nova at token weights. Color never carries meaning alone; labels, position, border weight, and motion direction reinforce each relationship.
