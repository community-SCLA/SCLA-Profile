# Design — Visibility Actions: Shared Evidence

## Selected concept

**Shared Evidence** replaces the rejected optical layout. Existing work is represented as bold white paper; intentional actions are gold; relationships and shared context are blue. The cut avoids camera chrome, persistent sidebars, giant title fields, repeated boxes, and template-like panels.

## Motion rules

- Use short from-to entrances with distinct horizontal direction and scale-pop choreography.
- Time listed actions to their word-level narration cues.
- Draw paths only after both connected objects are established.
- Hold text-bearing objects still after settlement.
- Fade scenes only after the narration sentence completes.
- Use one finite scale breathe on the quiet ripple; no repeating text or box motion.

## Type hierarchy

- Program and lesson masthead: 22px / 900.
- Scene headline: 58–66px / 900.
- Focal teaching phrase: 42–82px / 900.
- Body: 29–38px / 400 or 700.
- Label: 19–22px / 900 uppercase.

All values use `tokens.yml`; white, blue, and gold carry primary contrast on the deep navy field.
