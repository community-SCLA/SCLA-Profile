# Visibility, Made Visible

An editorial lesson built around a simple idea: visibility is not volume. A steady gold signal travels through eight focused beats, turning contributions into a practical three-action plan. The visual language uses clean cards, purposeful diagrams, and one central path rather than decorative motion.

## Story arc

1. Contrast networking with visibility.
2. Reframe visibility as useful context, not self-promotion.
3. Define small, intentional visibility actions.
4. Show a varied field of concrete examples.
5. Reassure quieter professionals that volume is not required.
6. Build a tailored set of three actions.
7. Select one 30-day experiment.
8. Resolve on a repeatable practice that invites support.

## Motion approach

One paused GSAP timeline drives every reveal. Cards rise and settle, connector lines draw from left to right, and the signal dot advances through the lesson. All movement is finite, seekable, and subordinate to the narration.
