# Visibility Actions — Shared Evidence

## Selected visual thesis

**Visibility is not a spotlight on the person. It is the deliberate act of making useful evidence easier for the right people to encounter and understand.**

## Visual language

The cut uses bold paper-cut illustrations rather than interface panels or a persistent camera metaphor. White paper represents existing work, blue establishes relationships and shared context, and gold marks the intentional action that makes evidence legible. People are drawn as simple two-part figures; paths appear only when they connect named evidence to named audiences.

Every idea owns its scene. Connections, evidence bridges, verb steps, action illustrations, a quiet ripple, three differently shaped practice sketches, and a 30-day reflection share one material language without forcing one container across the lesson.

## Milestone frames

1. Two people connect while a large sheet of useful work remains spatially separate.
2. Contribution and potential move into the view of decision-makers, sponsors, and peers.
3. A crossed-out megaphone yields to a bright sheet labeled “The Work.”
4. Three named audiences connect to work, growth, and value through clear relationship paths.
5. Place, Link, and Build rise as three different steps toward recognizable contribution.
6. Present, Share, Ask, Stretch, Mentor, and Teach appear as scene-owned illustrations timed to the narration.
7. A quiet gold ripple holds one useful moment while “Loud” and “Always On” stay peripheral.
8. Share, Ask, and Offer become a document, a speech bubble, and a teaching board rather than repeated cards.
9. One action, a 30-day calendar, and reflection form a three-part test.
10. Useful work and future direction connect to named supporters in the closing frame.

## Motion logic

- **Handoff:** evidence sheets move into a shared field with people.
- **Relationship draw:** short SVG paths connect only named states.
- **Meaningful arrival:** the item being narrated enters or sharpens at its word cue.
- **Illustrated transformation:** a megaphone is crossed out, a question reaches a meeting, and a calendar resolves into reflection.
- **Quiet settle:** the central ripple breathes once while surrounding performance language stays dim.

Motion is finite, deterministic, transform-based, and registered on one paused GSAP timeline. There are no progress rails, scene counters, false controls, detached focus boxes, static sidebars, or whole-scene transitions inside narration.

## Layout families

1. **People and evidence field** — asymmetrical connection and audience compositions.
2. **Contrast and bridge** — a visual contrast followed by named evidence-to-audience relationships.
3. **Verb path** — Place, Link, and Build rise through a spatial sequence.
4. **Action illustration field** — concrete action icons occupy distinct positions and activate with narration.
5. **Quiet signal** — a central restrained ripple contrasts with peripheral performance noise.
6. **Practice sketches** — document, speech bubble, teaching board, calendar, and reflection each receive a purpose-built composition.
7. **Support resolve** — evidence and future direction balance around a person and named supporters.

## Type and spacing

- Scene headlines: 58–66px, complete phrases, never used as giant illustration replacements.
- Teaching words: 42–82px only when they are the semantic focal object.
- Body copy: 29–38px.
- Labels: 19–22px uppercase.
- Main content fills the 1696×820 scene field while maintaining at least 40px between unrelated text, illustrations, and paths.
