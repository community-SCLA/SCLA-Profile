# Four Kinds of Career Transition

## Core visual idea

A living **transition compass** turns an abstract career decision into a map. The horizontal axis moves from adjacent to dramatic change; the vertical axis moves from chosen to forced change. Every section returns to this same map so the viewer always knows where they are.

## Story arc

1. Resume, application, and school tactics appear before the map pulls focus back to the first question: what kind of move is this?
2. The two axes draw on and resolve into four named transition types.
3. Growth Pivot expands within the chosen/adjacent quadrant.
4. Reinvention becomes a trail of accumulated adjacent steps, using Vera Wang's path as the example.
5. Rebuild shows transferable strengths moving from an unstable context to a healthier one.
6. Forced Reinvention shows an external disruption opening a fundamentally new route.
7. A response pathway reframes forced change as grief, support, strength transfer, and identity experiments.
8. A zoomed-out trail makes a dramatic leap legible as accumulated small moves.
9. The four quadrants become a practical tactics board.
10. The compass simplifies to one highlighted next step: name the transition, then choose the matching tactic.

## Visual language

- SCLA navy field with white paper panels.
- Blue defines the matrix and direction; gold identifies the active path.
- Large type, restrained labels, and simple geometric symbols keep the model readable at a glance.
- Motion is finite and seek-safe: axis drawing, card entrances, path traces, and emphasis pulses.
- No decorative footage or generic imagery; every visible element advances the model.
